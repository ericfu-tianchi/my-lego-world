"新天鹅堡一定要是绝绝对对的王者主角", plus one simple companion (a Bavarian village corner), and details such as the lake, swans and the bridge. Rule 13's "four or five places" is waived for this island; every other rule holds.

## 1. Research (before building)

### 1.1 Scene in one sentence
A floating island cut from the Alpine foothills: a **grey limestone crag** fills the back, and on it, **Neuschwanstein** stretches along the ridge — the white Palas with its steep slate roof and gold-ochre dormers at the left, the needle-spired **stair tower** rising far above it, the **square tower** with its round crenellated crown, and the low **red-brick gatehouse** at the right end; a road climbs the crag to the gate. To the right a narrow **gorge** drops a white **waterfall** under the thin iron **Marienbrücke**. At the foot of the crag the valley opens: the calm grey-blue **Alpsee** with two **swans** at the front-left, a green meadow at the front-right with one **Allgäu farmhouse** (white render, timber balcony, red geraniums), a blue-and-white **maypole**, a brown cow, and a **horse carriage** waiting where the road starts. Late spring: white castle on dark spruce, fresh green meadow.

### 1.5 Scale, footprints, tiers (first estimate; the stud-grid sketches at the landmark checkpoint set the final numbers)
**Scale rule — the crag is the scale boundary.** Everything on the crag (castle, road, bridge, crag trees) is castle scale, ≈ 1.25 × 21063; everything on the valley floor (lake, swans, farmhouse, maypole, carriage, cow, figures, valley trees) is minifig scale. No minifig stands on the crag. At castle scale a minifig would be two storeys tall; keeping them below the cliff is what lets the castle stay the king. The real place does the same: the carriage stops below the castle.

### 1.7 Lettering culture (rule 1, L2)
- Bavarian village lettering is **Fraktur** (blackletter) painted on render or wrought into iron inn signs, and Lüftlmalerei's painted frames. Recorded choice: **no lettering in this set**. The maypole's guild signs are pictorial (a cartwheel, a pretzel, a beer barrel, a horse) in ≤ 4 flat colours on white boards, exactly as the real ones; an inn name or a painted year would not read at thumbnail size.

## 2. Build notes (country-specific techniques)
- **Files**: `src/scenes/de-castle.ts` (the castle, `NS_COL` palette), `de-mason.ts` (wall cells → run packer, arched windows), `de-valley.ts` (farmhouse, maypole, carriage, Marienbrücke), `neuschwanstein.ts` (the island). Test scenes `?scene=de-ns` (castle on a plate) and `de-col-a|b|c` (roof palettes).
- **Windows**: a 3-plate opening plus a 3-plate row whose piers are half-arch bricks — 38583 between two openings, 38585 at a group's end with its second half turned into the building. Glass sits **two** studs in: one stud in, the rectangular box of a group-end 38585 takes the glass at its diagonal. Group ends never sit next to a corner pier of the other wall (the two 38585 boxes collide).
- **Crag**: a hollow shell. Face columns are solid from the floor; the plateau's slab (t−4..t−2) is laid by our own runs along z on every plateau column, the deck plates above along x, so the two cross and the deck hangs from the faces. Tiles in the deck broke the runs into islands of loose bricks; the deck is all plates and the courtyard paving / grass sits on top of it. A bare-rock band (9-plate risers clad in 75° slopes, exact 2-stud treads) under the walls in patches, forested ledges (6 plates per 2 studs) elsewhere, castle-scale spruces = a Dark Green jagged 2×2 cone (+ a 1×1 cone).
- **Colour** (2026-10-06): a first pass to Sand Blue roofs read as a toy (Eric: "太像迪士尼…儿童玩具"; the judge: too light, purple). Final: **21063's own palette** — Black roofs and cones (our renderer's black is a deep blue-black, the sunlit slate), Dark Bluish Gray string courses, Dark Tan dormer faces, Tan gallery crowns, **Dark Red** gatehouse.
- **Water**: Kyoto's recipe — a coloured bed under light-blue tiles (deep blue middle, azure toward the shore), an opaque Light Aqua rim; trans tiles over a pale or very dark bed rendered as ice. The waterfall is a 2-wide column of clear / light-blue bricks against the gorge head.
- **Swans** are white ducklings (the Prague lesson: no swan mould in LDraw, brick-built swans failed twice there).
