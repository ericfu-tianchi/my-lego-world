## 1. Research (before building)

### 1.1 Scene in one sentence
A floating island on **the Old Town bank of the Vltava**: the dark Gothic **Old Town Bridge Tower** guards the end of a short run of **Charles Bridge** whose low sandstone arches and black statues cross the river to a small green Kampa bank; behind, on a cobbled square, the twin many-pointed slate spires of **Týn Church** (the tallest thing) rise over a row of cream gabled houses, and the tan **Old Town Hall tower** carries the **Astronomical Clock**. A violinist plays on the bridge, a visitor waits for the clock to strike, a painter works by the river; two swans. Late-summer day: warm sandstone, slate, terracotta.

### 1.4 Palette (LEGO names, from the photos)
- Stone: **Tan** with **Dark Tan** mottling (Orloj tower, bridge, Týn shafts with **Light Bluish Gray**). The museum model and the 21061 review agree: Prague sandstone is tan, not grey.
- Blackened stone: **Dark Brown + Dark Bluish Gray + Black** mottle (bridge tower, Orloj frame).
- Roofs: **Dark Bluish Gray / Black** slate (towers, spires); **Dark Orange** terracotta with a few **Dark Nougat** tiles (houses).
- Plaster: **Bright Light Yellow**, **Light Nougat**, **White**; white stucco frames.
- Accents: **Pearl Gold** (spire balls, Orloj, Madonna), **Sand Green** copper (Orloj hood, one dome or dormer), Dark Green lamps, red flower boxes (**Red**, sparingly).
- Water: Dark Blue / Sand Blue under Trans-Light-Blue. Grass: the series' two greens.
- **Not**: grey as sandstone (reads as concrete); Bright Red or vermilion as a primary (Kyoto); pastels louder than Light Nougat (no Bright Pink / Medium Azure — Singapore's shophouse colours); Reddish Orange roofs (too loud next to Kyoto); Dark Turquoise.

### 1.7 Lettering culture (rule 1, L2)
- Street plates: **red enamel, white bold sans capitals, white border with stepped corners** — iconic, but a street name is too long to read at thumbnail → not used.
- **House signs** (domovní znamení): carved or painted emblems over the door, often gilded — "U Zlatého klíče" (At the Golden Key). One gold key on a dark red cartouche, **no text** = readable and true. Recorded choice: emblem only, painted style, ≤ 3 flat colours.
- Orloj dials carry Roman / Schwabacher numerals — drawn as part of the dial print, painted-gold style, never a UI typeface.
- No shop lettering elsewhere.
