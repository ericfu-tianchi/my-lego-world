## 2. Build notes
- **Lettering**: a 扁額 is dark lacquer with gold carved-stone script (`PLAQUE` stack: Weibei SC → Toppan Bunkyu Midashi Mincho → Baoli SC); a paper 提灯 carries one brush character in ink red (`BRUSH` stack: Baoli SC → Klee). Typeset Mincho read as a modern shop sign; cream board + black text vanished in eave shadow. One or two characters only.
- **Torii proportions**: kasagi 10 > nuki 8 > shimagi 6 studs; pillars 5 round bricks; upturn = 1×2 curved slope one stud past the beam each side.
- **Pagoda**: three mirror-symmetric bays per side (pillar k pairs with S−1−k); eave tips flick outward along x on both sides; plinth of two plate layers.
- **Tea house**: 8×6, walls 7 bricks, minifig lattice door centred, plaque brick above the lintel, engawa deck two plates up with a two-plate step, two lantern posts at the deck corners, low table with cups, the cat on the deck.
- **Karesansui**: 10×8 white tiles with alternate rows of light-grey grille tiles, kerb of Dark Bluish Gray plates, rocks = 2×2 brick + dome, slope, nugget; the monk stands on the kerb so the broom head rests on the sand.
- **Waterfall**: stream lip → trans sheet down the plinth face → a small trans pool on the table + three white foam pieces.
- **Island**: 56×44, base 3 plates up (floating), rim wall to the table, two-brick stone plinth one cell outside, capped with tiles; pond as the calm centre; continuous stepping-stone line torii → bridge → tea-house step.
