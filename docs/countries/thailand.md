## 1. Research (before building)

### 1.1 Scene in one sentence
A floating island shaped like **Pattaya's own coastline — a crescent wrapped round a calm bay that opens to the viewer** (the real bay faces west, the camera stands at sea): on the left horn, the north headland of Naklua, the all-wooden **Sanctuary of Truth** rises behind a pale crenellated seawall with sea on three sides — a pyramid of sweeping, stacked Thai roofs crowned by five spires, brown carved walls under silver-grey roofs, one blue-tarped scaffold on a corner because it is never finished; the middle is the long Tan crescent of **Pattaya Beach** with blocks of umbrellas, a grey promenade with red-and-white kerbs and one row of coconut palms, and Beach Road behind it with a **seafood grill stall** under a blue tarp and a blue **songthaew**; on the right horn the jungle-green **Pratumnak Hill** carries the red **PATTAYA** letters facing the bay; in the bay a white **speedboat** has stopped over a turquoise reef patch and a snorkeller in an orange life jacket is going in. Hot dry-season afternoon: grey-green sea, tan sand, brown wood, one red sign.

### 1.4 Palette (LEGO names, from the photos)

### 1.7 Lettering culture (rule 1, L2)
- **PATTAYA**: the real sign is a geometric sans in red 3-D box letters → **brick-built**, no print; geometry from the real letterforms (straight-legged A, symmetric Y), 3 × 5 pixels.
- **"city"**: white lowercase script on a steel frame → one printed tile, white script on Dark Bluish Gray, `px ≥ 320`; cut if it does not read at thumbnail.
- **Stall banner**: Pattaya stalls hang printed vinyl banners with **bold looped Thai display lettering**, white on red or red on white, often with the word **ซีฟู้ด** ("seafood"). Choice: one banner tile, **ซีฟู้ด** in a heavy looped Thai face (Sukhumvit Set Bold / Thonburi Bold, system fonts on macOS — fallback checked), White on Red, nothing else; no invented shop name.
- No Thai lettering on the Sanctuary (the real building has none).

### 1.13 Eric on the Sanctuary after judge round 3 (2026-10-06)
- **The soul is the upsweep**: every eave curves up into its ends ("每一个屋檐其实都是往上翘的…横平竖直，缺少了那个感觉"). Not optional.
- **Monumental proportion**: the wings and side halls are longer and more exaggerated than ours; it must feel overwhelming ("非常非常震撼"), not like an ordinary building. It may grow and use more parts.
- **Intricacy**: what makes it is the carving and the figures, large and small. LEGO cannot copy it, but many small detail parts must make it read as dense and ornate ("繁复").
- Response: plan grown to 40 × 40 (v4, ≈ 2,900 parts, budget raised to ≈ 4,000 within the set's 8,000); stepped-plate upsweep tried and reads as a staircase → technique study (upswept eaves in official Asian-roof sets and MOCs, ornate greebling parts) before the next build round.

## 2. Build notes (country-specific techniques)
- **Sanctuary — the swept wings** (Eric approved the form 2026-10-06: "这个形态我已经满意了"). Each wing roof beyond the crossing is two rigid sub-builds that hang on clip hinges at the ridge: a plate with a handle (2540) on the fixed side, a plate with two clips (60470a) on the swept side, handle and clips meeting over an empty ridge cell. Inner hinges sit just outside the crossing's face gables (cells 11 / 28), outer ones five cells further (6 / 33); tilt 5° then +7°. Turning about the ridge opens a wedge crack at the eave instead of crushing the ridge (a bottom pivot made the top fibres collide); Eric accepts the cracks as long as the build is real. The wall ring under the lifted roof rises in whole plates, tiled on top. The voxel packer never joins a brick across a hinge line (`segOf`). Clip axis is 2 LDU above the handle bar when both plates sit at one level — accepted as clip flex.
- Stepped-plate lift at the eave ends (1–3 plates) was tried first and read as a staircase; kept in `roof()` as `sweepCurve`, off.
- Eave ends: a curled vine (1997) on the lowest tier, a curved blade (87747) above, both on open-stud round plates; gable apex: a curved flame (18395). Chosen from a five-candidate strip render (87747, 11089, 1997, 28870, 18395).
