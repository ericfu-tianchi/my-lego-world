## 2. Pipeline changes (owned here)
- **Wrist turn** (`tools/ldraw/minifig-asm.mjs`): the hand turns on its wrist pin (3820's 4-4cylc on hand-local z), `Rz(90°)` on the hand's own matrix. The grip point moves ≤ 0.05 stud; the clip's bore swings from (vertical / front-to-back / 45°, by arm pose) to **left-right in every pose**. Hands keyed `fig_hands_<L><R>_<wL><wR>` (t = turned). Verified in the test scene (dumbbell across a turned hand in h and f: hand closed on the bar, PASS) and then dropped from the scene — see §3.
- **Two more arm angles**: **b** +45° (arm swung back until the forearm hangs plumb: the clip runs front-to-back — a board or bag carried at the side) and **k** +90° (upper arm straight back: a balance / stride arm). Torsos and hands beyond the base sixteen are baked only where used (`EXTRA_TORSO`, `EXTRA_HANDS`; now kf, fk) to keep the app small; the catalogue registers whatever `RIG.extra` lists.
- **RIG.axis** (clip bore per side × pose × wrist) and `HOLD_DEFAULTS[key].axis`: `minifig2` now throws `grip axis` when a prop's rail / bar does not lie along the clip. Existing holds declare no axis, so no existing figure changed.
- **Pre-turned held props** `figheld_<name>.ldr` (one real part each, baked as one mesh): `surfboard_up`.
- `minifig2({ skates: colour })`: a roller skate under each foot hole, the figure one plate higher.
- The rig bake no longer leaves member lists / stray moulds behind (bake.mjs treats every .ldr as an assembly; minifig-asm cleans `asm/fig*` and moulds queued only for them).
- Gate scripts (shared, two tokens): `check-assemblies` INTERLOCK `42511|2496` and `check-asm-studs` BY_DESIGN `42511` — the skateboard deck is held by its clips on the wheel axles, not by studs.

## 5. Notes for the other elements
- **Skater at the bowl**: needs a flat 6 × 3 stud strip at the coping: a **1 × 2 riser of two plates** (studs exposed) under the board's centre, the board along the coping's normal; the riser cell nearest the bowl at the coping edge puts the front wheels over the drop (board ±2.5 studs). Figure = board top + 1 plate.
- **Roller-skater** needs a **studded** patch of walk (2 studs under the feet, skates straddle them): tiles will not do.
- **Surfer** needs studs under both feet and a clear 2 × 1 patch of sand at his front-right for the board's tail (centre ≈ 1.9 studs to his right, 1.6 in front); the board stands 17 plates tall. Face him to the main camera (the deck faces his front).
- Palm: `fanPalm(bp, x, y, z, { turn })` with (x, z) a grid corner, ≈ 40 plates tall; reserve 4 × 4, head Ø ≈ 6 → pitch 8 (`Sites` radius 3); a row of 6 spans ≈ 46 studs. Ground under the jumper must be studded (a Tan 4 × 4 pocket in tiles works).
- Catalogue: `bamboo_leaves` (existing, hand-made primitive for 30176) should be replaced by the real mould `plant_bamboo_1x1` — not changed here.
