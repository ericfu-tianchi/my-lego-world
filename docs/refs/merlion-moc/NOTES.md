# Merlion in bricks — reference notes (gathered 2026-09-19)

Purpose: how other builders make a small Merlion *read* as the Merlion, plus real-statue photos for proportion.
Part names are types, not numbers, unless the source gave numbers. Rebrickable pages are Cloudflare-gated; only the
CDN hero image of each MOC could be pulled, so stud/brick sizes below are estimated from the render unless stated.

## 0. Real statue — proportions to hit (Merlion Park, 8.6 m tall, white/cream)
Files: `real-merlion-front-closeup-wikimedia.jpg` (head detail), `real-merlion-front-full-body-wikimedia.jpg`
(front 3/4, full body + wave base), `real-merlion-side-profile-night-wikimedia.jpg` (near-pure left profile),
`real-merlion-side-3q-blue-hour-brothersbrick.jpg` (left 3/4 with MBS; photo credit William Lum via Brothers Brick),
`real-merlion-rear-mbs-wikimedia.jpg` (rear 3/4).
- Silhouette in profile: a **C-curve** — head cantilevers forward over the chest, tail curls forward at the foot. Body
  height ≈ 3 × head height; head depth ≈ body width. Mane falls straight down the back as **vertical ridges**, ending
  in a flat-cut hem about one head below the chin.
- Head: broad flat brow, small pricked ears, open mouth with bared teeth; jet leaves horizontally from the mouth
  (a pipe, so the stream is a thin horizontal rod first, then an arc).
- Body: fish scales in overlapping **fan / arch rows**; chest has a pair of small fins; a **round hole** through the
  tail-fin curl is a signature detail visible in every side view.
- Base: a low **blue-and-white striped wave plinth**, asymmetrical, sitting on a grey stone drum — the only colour on
  the monument. Statue ≈ 2 × base width; base ≈ 1/6 of total height.
- Note: `Merlion_Profile_Singapore.JPG` on Commons is the 37 m **Sentosa** Merlion (hexagonal scales, demolished
  2019) — wrong statue for proportion; discarded.

## 1. Rebrickable MOC-193738 "Singapore Merlion" — brickbicycle
https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-193738/ · file `rebrickable-moc-193738-brickbicycle-merlion-main.png`
- Render on a 8×8 light-grey plate; body ≈ 4 studs wide, ≈ 14 bricks to the ear tips. Minifig-adjacent scale (no fig).
- Head/mane: stacked white bricks with **wedge plates and 45° slopes** for the muzzle, two **1×1 round plates / tile
  eyes** (dark grey), **cheese slopes** as ears, mane as **layered plates stepped back** down the spine.
- Mouth/jet: a **1×1 round brick** pushed out of the mouth as the jet nozzle; pink 1×1 tile as the tongue.
- Body scales: column of **1×2 curved slopes** / **1×1 slopes** set in a zig-zag on the back and a **1×1 round brick
  column** on the flank; front chest is smooth bricks with **plate-with-clip** fins.
- Tail: a **2×2 round / curved bottom** at the foot; a small **wedge plate** for the tail fin.
- Reads as Merlion because: forward-leaning lion head + one straight column of scale texture + white on grey.

## 2. Rebrickable MOC-86021 "Merlion Park" — Wadjetik
https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-86021/ · file `rebrickable-moc-86021-wadjetik-merlion-park-main.png`
- Minifig scale diorama ("a simple model of the Merlion Park"): statue ≈ 12 bricks tall, 3 studs wide, on a
  **dark-grey 2×2 round plate** plinth, with a viewing deck (dark-grey plates on arches) and one minifig.
- Head/mane: **1×1 clip plates / hinge plates / minifig-arm-like bar pieces** fanned as a loose mane; the head is a
  small SNOT block with headlight bricks. Very open, "wire" mane.
- Mouth/jet: a **chain of 1×1 round plates / bars** drawn out of the mouth down to trans-clear **1×1 round plates**
  and **trans-black windscreens** as splash in the pond.
- Body: stacked **2×2 round bricks / 1×1 round bricks** and **curved slopes** giving a knobbly scaled column.
- Tail: **hinge plates and 1×2 plates** angled out to one side.
- Lesson: reads at a glance mainly through the arc of the water into a pool; the mane is too spiky/open.

## 3. Rebrickable MOC-23376 "Merlion Microscale" — Lioncity Mocs (2018, 18 parts)
https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-23376/ · file `rebrickable-moc-23376-lioncity-merlion-microscale-main.png`
- **18 parts**, ≈ 2 studs wide × 6 bricks tall on a **dark-grey 2×2 plate + 2×2 round plate**.
- Head/mane: one **2×2 curved slope (double curved)** as the whole head; a **1×1 round plate** eye on each side; the
  mane is a **1×1 slope** on the neck.
- Mouth/jet: a **1×1 round plate with bar / lever** in the face, carrying a **trans-light-blue flame / wave**
  element as the water jet — instant read.
- Body: **1×2 curved slope + 1×1 slope** for the chest; **2×2 round brick / macaroni** feel at the bottom.
- Tail: a **wedge plate** kicked out to the back at the foot.
- Lesson: at micro scale, three things carry the read — white, a forward-leaning curved head, and a horizontal
  trans-blue jet. Good target for a 56×44 island landmark (we can go ~2× this).

## 4. Rebrickable MOC-155340 "Merlion Fountain Statue" (Ninjago City expansion) — DonnaxNL
https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-155340/ · file `rebrickable-moc-155340-donnaxnl-merlion-fountain-main.jpg`
- Photo of the real model: ≈ 6 studs wide, ≈ 15 bricks tall, on a light-grey stepped promenade with a
  **light-grey 4×4 round quarter** drum and a **blue/white wave plinth**; trans-light-blue tiles as pool.
- Head/mane: **cheese slopes and 1×2 curved slopes** stepped down the back for the mane, **cheese slopes as ears**,
  a **1×1 round tile eye**; the muzzle is **1×2 curved slopes** on SNOT.
- Mouth/jet: a **1×1 round plate with bar** exits the mouth; jet is a curved run of **trans-light-blue 1×1 round
  bricks / cones / flexible tube segments** arcing into a **trans-blue flower-stud splash** in the pool.
- Body scales: **white flower plates (1×1 flower), 1×1 round plates and small curved slopes** hung on SNOT bricks
  facing outward — this gives the overlapping scale texture better than plain bricks.
- Tail: **curved wedges / 1×2 curved slopes** curling forward at the foot.
- Lesson: flower plates + round plates read as fish scales at any distance; water arc with a splash puddle sells it.

## 5. Bricks World / LEGO Certified Store — Ethan Yee (@bricksgowhere) competition Merlion, RWS Sentosa diorama

- Larger than minifig scale (fig ≈ 1/4 statue height): ≈ 8 studs wide, ≈ 20 bricks tall, white, on a black oval
  base ringed with 1×1 round plates as flowers.
- Body: **studs-on-all-four-sides core** (BrickHeadz method — SNOT bricks/brackets outward), then plates hung on
  it, with **1×1 round plates / cheese slopes** as scales.
- Mane: **long curved slopes and wedges** (from 10234 Sydney Opera House) laid vertically as ridges, tips
  overhanging the chest; mouth open with a **1×1 slope teeth** row. Head built first, then body, tail, base.
- Tail: designed from a pencil sketch — a **curved wedge slope** curling forward with a **cheese slope** fin.
- Lesson: the vertical-ridge mane + forward lean is what makes it "the Merlion" from any angle; jet omitted.
- Not found: a "LEGO Certified Store Merlion" exclusive set / item 6315786 — searches return only the Suntec
  Merlion mosaic wall (92,160 pieces), a 2023 Changi Airport "Merlion Microbuild Make & Take" event with no
  published photo/instructions, and the RWS diorama above. **21057 Singapore Skyline contains no Merlion.**

## 6. LEGOLAND Malaysia — MINILAND Singapore Merlion (relaunched Aug 2025)
https://www.legoland.com.my/explore/theme-park/rides-attractions/miniland-singapore/ · file
`legoland-malaysia-miniland-merlion-fountain.jpg`
- Miniland scale (figs ≈ 4 bricks tall): statue ≈ 10 figs high, on a **striped blue/white wave plinth** and a
  **light-grey round drum** at the water's edge; pumps a real water jet on button press.
- Built the LEGOLAND way: **stacked plates/bricks only**, all white, curves from stepped plate outlines; the mane is
  a plain rounded slab behind the head, eyes are **black 1×1 round plates**, mouth a dark recess.
- Reads because of silhouette + wave plinth + jet, not detail. Good calibration of "minimum detail that still reads".

## Takeaways for our island (Kyoto scale, landmark ≤ 0.6 × island width, three tiers)
1. Non-negotiables for the read: white body; head leaning forward over the chest; **horizontal trans-light-blue
   jet** from the mouth into a splash; blue/white wave plinth on a grey drum.
2. Mane = vertical ridges (curved slopes / plates on edge), not spikes. Scales = **flower plates / 1×1 round plates
   on SNOT**, or omit at micro scale.
3. Tail curls forward at the foot with a fin; the hole through the tail curl is a bonus if scale permits.
4. Micro target ≈ 4 studs wide × 10–12 bricks tall reads well (between MOC-23376 and MOC-155340).
