# Marina Bay Sands — LEGO references

Collected 2026-09-19. Part names are types, not numbers, unless the source gave one.

## Real thing (for proportions and colour)

- `real-mbs-merlion-park-commons-ankkumar02.jpg`, `real-mbs-merlion-park-commons-ankkumar04.jpg` — from Merlion Park across the bay, with ArtScience Museum and the Flyer. Wikimedia Commons (Ank Kumar). https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marina_Bay_Sands_view_from_Merlion_Park,_Singapore_(Ank_Kumar)_04.jpg
- `real-mbs-jays-photo.jpg` — Jay's own straight-on photo of the three towers (best single shot of the "deck of cards" lean). https://jaysbrickblog.com/reviews/review-lego-architecture-21057-singapore-guest-review/
- `real-mbs-tbb-merlion.jpg` — Brothers Brick's reference photo. https://www.brothers-brick.com/2021/12/04/lego-architecture-21057-singapore-review/

What to copy: three **55-storey slab towers**, each a flat glass slab with a **white concrete edge frame**. Each tower is really *two leaves*: a vertical inner leaf and an outer leaf that **leans outward at the base and meets the inner one near the top** — so the foot of every tower is wider than its head and there is a sliver of daylight between the leaves low down (very visible on the left tower). Glass is **pale blue-grey / greenish, never saturated blue**. The **SkyPark** is a long white boat-hull deck sitting *across* the three heads, longer than the towers' spread; it **cantilevers ~65 m past the north tower** (the overhang is about one tower-width) and tapers to a point. On top: **infinity pool** at the cantilever end (reads as a pale-blue strip), a row of trees (dark green dots) in the middle, two small white pavilions. Below: a low podium of white shell-roofs (convention centre) and the ArtScience "lotus" nearby. Proportion: tower height : SkyPark length ≈ 1 : 1.7; tower width : tower height ≈ 1 : 4.

## Build 1 — LEGO Architecture 21057 Singapore (official, 2022)

Files: `21057-jays-mbs-front.jpg`, `21057-jays-mbs-back.jpg`, `21057-jays-mbs-side.jpg`, `21057-jays-mbs-rooftop.jpg`, `21057-tbb-mbs-30.jpg` … `21057-tbb-mbs-34.jpg`
Sources: Jay's Brick Blog (above); Brothers Brick (above)

- Size: whole MBS ~22 studs wide, towers 4 studs wide × 2 deep, ~12 bricks tall; SkyPark 24 studs long.
- Tower lean: each tower is a **hollow white frame** of 1×2 bricks / 1×1 bricks with side studs around a **1×2 Technic-brick / 1×1-with-side-stud spine**; the outer leaf flares at the base with **stepped white slopes / plates** so the foot is wider (visible in `front.jpg`). Two **"blaster" / clip-bar elements angled 45°** brace the top of each tower to the SkyPark (`tbb-32.jpg`). Base: **1×2 grille tiles in white** as the podium colonnade.
- Glass facing: **trans-dark-blue 1×4 plates / 1×2 tiles stacked SNOT** on the front face, held on the side studs; back face is **1×2 white grille tiles** as balconies (`back.jpg`). Reviewers' verdict: the blue is too saturated and the towers too blocky.
- SkyPark deck: three **white segments** hinged so the deck **kinks twice in plan** — the two end segments angle back like a boat hull (`tbb-34.jpg` shows top / bottom). Underside carries **light-blue 2×2 round / 2×4 plates with pin holes** that seat on the tower tops. Ends are **2×2 round tiles / curved slopes** in white for the hull rounding.
- Cantilever: the north segment simply **projects one tower-width past the last tower**, unsupported — the hinge plus the tower-top pin is enough at this size.
- Pool: **trans-light-blue 1×2 tiles** running along the deck's inner edge (the long side) as a strip; **light-bluish-grey 2×2 round tiles / 2×2 tiles** as the two pavilions; **green 1×1 round plates (flower-stud style)** as the tree row (`rooftop.jpg`).

## Build 2 — LEGO Architecture 21021 Marina Bay Sands (official, 2013, 602 pcs, MBS only)

Files: `21021-newelementary-complete.jpg`, `21021-newelementary-tower-sideways.jpg`, `21021-newelementary-skypark-snot.jpg`, `21021-brickset-front.jpg`, `21021-brickset-cantilever.jpg`, `21021-brickset-rear-grilles.jpg`, `21021-brickset-skypark-gardens.jpg`, `21021-brickset-tower-fronts-snot.jpg`
Sources: New Elementary https://www.newelementary.com/2014/01/return-of-trans-light-blue.html ; Brickset review https://brickset.com/article/8966/review-21021-marina-bay-sands

- Size: ~24 studs wide, towers 6 studs wide, ~16 bricks tall; SkyPark ~28 studs long.
- Tower lean: the best reference for the curve. Each tower's outer leaf is **stacked white plates stepping outward one plate-step at a time** ("5:2 technique", 1×1 plate stacking, every tower slightly different — New Elementary). From the side the stepped white edge reads as the outward lean; the inner leaf is vertical. The three **fronts are built as separate sub-models attached SNOT** onto the tower core ("ingenious SNOT" — Brickset), using **clip-lights to reverse 180°** inside hollow cores.
- Glass facing: **trans-light-blue 1×2 plates (98), 1×1 plates (19) and 1×4 tiles (39)** stacked sideways; rear face is **white 1×2 grille tiles** for balconies (`rear-grilles.jpg`). Trans-light-blue is the *right* glass colour — closer to the real pale blue-green than 21057's dark blue.
- SkyPark deck: a **white plate hull with curved-slope ends**, gardens as **olive-green 1×1 round tiles** (`skypark-gardens.jpg`); pool not explicitly modelled.
- Cantilever: **two white 1×2/1×2 inverted brackets** anchor the projecting end (`cantilever.jpg` shows the trans-light-blue stepped base under the north tower and the deck end overhanging one tower-width).

## Build 3 — Rebrickable MOC-84745 "Marina Bay Sands" (Wadjetik, micro)

File: `moc-84745-wadjetik-mbs-artscience.png`
Source: https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-84745/Wadjetik/marina-bay-sands/

- Size: ~16×8 studs incl. ArtScience Museum; towers 2×2 footprint, 4 bricks tall.
- Towers: **2×2 round brick (white) → 1×2 brick dark-grey → 1×2 trans-black brick → 1×2 white brick** stacked; the round brick at the base gives the flared-foot reading cheaply.
- SkyPark: a single **1×16 black plate** laid across the three tower tops, projecting 3 studs past the last tower; two **1×2 white tiles** as pavilions. Pool not built.
- ArtScience Museum: **eight 1×1 bricks with side studs on a ring of clips** around a 2×2 round core — a fan of "petals"; water as **medium-blue 1×2 cheese slopes**.

## Build 4 — LEGOLAND Malaysia MINILAND Singapore (2025, 4 m tall)

Files: `miniland-legoland-my-mbs-1.png`, `miniland-legoland-my-mbs-2.png`
Source: https://thesmartlocal.com/read/legoland-malaysia-miniland-singapore/

- The large-scale answer key: towers are **trans-teal / dark-turquoise glass slabs edged in white**, the outer leaf visibly **leans outward at the foot** with a tan / white stepped frame; the SkyPark is a **white hull with a rounded taper** that overhangs the north tower by one tower width with a **row of dark-green trees and the pavilions on top**; podium is a strip of white shell roofs with minifig life (buses, crowds) at the base.

## Takeaways for a 56×44-stud island (landmark ≤ 0.6 × island width, minifig life in front)

1. **Budget the footprint from the SkyPark, not the towers.** SkyPark length = ~1.7 × tower height and it must overhang one tower-width. At the 33-stud cap: towers 4 wide × 2 deep, 6-stud gaps, three towers spanning 24 studs, SkyPark 30 studs long with a 6-stud cantilever — that is the whole allowance, so nothing else stands on MBS's axis.
2. **Lean = stepped outer leaf, not an angled part.** Copy 21021: inner leaf vertical, outer leaf **white plates stepping out one stud per 3–4 plates toward the base**, flat glass in between. Because our grid is integer studs, the lean is at most two steps (foot 2 studs wider than head); that is enough to read from the bay-side view. No hinges, no SNOT angles.
3. **Glass colour is trans-light-blue (or trans-clear over medium-azure), never trans-dark-blue.** Both reviews fault 21057's saturated blue; 21021's pale blue and Miniland's teal read as MBS. Keep the white edge frame one stud wide on every side so the towers read as framed slabs, not blue columns.
4. **The signature detail is the pool at the cantilever tip.** Build the SkyPark as a **white plate hull** (2×2 curved-slope / 2×2 round ends), then: **trans-light-blue tiles as a 2-stud-wide, 6–8-stud-long strip at the overhanging end**, a **row of 1×1 round green plates** mid-deck, two **light-grey 1×2 tiles** as pavilions. If the pool cannot be read at thumbnail size the SkyPark has failed (rule 6: cut what cannot be read).
5. **Cantilever must be structurally honest.** One tower-width of plate hull overhanging with nothing under it is what every reference does; keep the deck ≥ 2 plates thick and register it in the validator as connected through the tower tops only. The podium (white shell roofs) is a low 1-plate strip in front — that strip is where the minifig-scale life goes, and it keeps figures off the tower axis.
