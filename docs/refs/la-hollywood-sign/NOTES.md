**Published figures** (en.wikipedia / press, via search): 9 letters, each **45 ft (13.7 m) tall, 31–39 ft wide**, total length **≈ 350 ft (107 m)**, rebuilt 1978 in steel. The 1923 original said HOLLYWOODLAND, 50 ft letters; LAND removed 1949.

**Measured from `real-straight-on.jpg`** (white-pixel scan, letter face height H ≈ 100 px ≈ 45 ft, so 0.45 ft/px; the view is slightly oblique, so the left letters come out narrower):

| Feature | Photo | ÷ letter height H | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total length / letter height | 793 px / 100 px | **7.9 H** | matches published 350/45 = 7.8 |
| Letter width, H O L L D | 71–80 px | **0.7–0.8 H** | O, D widest of the regular letters |
| W | ≈ 100 px | **≈ 1.0 H** | the only letter wider than tall-ish; ≈ 1.3 × the others |
| Y | ≈ 62 px | ≈ 0.6 H | narrowest |
| Stroke width | 23 px (H stem) | **≈ 0.23 H** | so a letter is ≈ stroke · counter · stroke = 3 equal columns |
| O counter | 24 px wide | ≈ 0.24 H | tall slot, not a circle |
| Gaps between letters | 6–31 px, mean ≈ 15 | **≈ 0.15 H** | irregular: letters sit at different depths on the slope |
| Rise of letter tops H → D | ≈ 45 px | **≈ 0.45 H** | the line climbs left-to-right with the contour; the 2nd O dips |
| Legs under the letters | visible 0–0.2 H | | letters stand on poles; downhill side shows more leg |

- **Shape:** blocky sans-serif, flat faces, no outline. O and D are rounded rectangles with tall slot counters; W is two Vs (four strokes); Y has a short stem. Letter faces are **white**, backs are white-painted panels on a visible grey steel frame (girts every ≈ 0.2 H, two to four legs per letter, diagonal braces — `real-behind-scaffold.jpg`).
- **Site:** south face of Mount Lee, just below the summit. Letters stand **in one row along a contour path** with a chain-link fence behind (`real-hill-view.jpg`). The **summit antenna** (red/white lattice mast with white dish clusters, `real-straight-on.jpg`) stands up-ridge to the right (east) of the D; from the basin it is the second half of the silhouette (`real-from-basin.jpg`).
- **How it reads from the basin:** a short bright white strip on a dark brown hill, under the crest, with the mast as a vertical accent. Nothing else on the hill reads. That is the whole signature: **white blocks in a line on dark chaparral**.

## 1. Brickman "Wonders of the World", Ryan McNaught (2016), minifig scale — the best reference
URL: https://www.flickr.com/photos/ryanmcnaught/30555855763/ · https://www.flickr.com/photos/porkynz/41986890092/ · https://www.flickr.com/photos/porkynz/41311002044/ · files `brickman-ryanmcnaught-front.jpg`, `brickman-sandyem-oblique.jpg`, `brickman-sandyem-hill-detail.jpg`
- "Just over 8,500 bricks" for the letters (Flickr caption); graffiti on the backs.
- **Letters:** White bricks **studs-up**, letter face = brick sides, 1–2 studs thick. Letters ≈ 6 minifigs tall. Rounded corners of O/D are 1-stud stair steps. Counters are open slots. W built as stepped diagonals.
- **Legs:** Light Bluish Gray **bars/poles** under each letter and grey cross-braces visible behind — the scaffold is the thing that makes it read as the real sign and not a word on the ground.
- **Hill:** a single slope of **Dark Tan** plates, terraced at plate steps, with Tan accents; dried-grass plants (Dark Tan/Reddish Brown spiky plants) and green bushes sparse; a trail of LBG / white / DBG 1×1 round plates as gravel; a ranger truck and minifigs for scale.
- Reads well: the letter proportions are close to real (≈ 0.75 H wide, 3-column stroke/counter/stroke), legs + bracing visible, hill colour right. Weak: hill is a flat ramp, no chaparral texture, no mast.
- **Lesson:** studs-up white letters on grey legs over a Dark Tan slope is the proven minifig-scale build. Copy the 3-column letter grid and the visible legs/bracing.

## 2. LEGOLAND California Miniland, "LEGOWOOD" (since 1999 [M]), Merlin model shop
URL: https://www.flickr.com/photos/n_nburke/6790269107/ · https://www.flickr.com/photos/mrphancy/259478350/ · https://www.flickr.com/photos/punkoid/49981602547/ · https://www.flickr.com/photos/atwatervillage/3614247951/ · files `miniland-ca-legowood-*.jpg`
- **LEGO's own park spells it LEGOWOOD**, not HOLLYWOOD — almost certainly to avoid the trademark (see §Risks).
- **Letters:** White bricks studs-up, ≈ 3 minifigs tall, ≈ 1 stud thick, **no legs** — pushed straight into the turf, slightly leaning, stepping up the slope. O/D/G rounded by stepped bricks.
- **Site:** set on the real-turf hill directly below the Griffith Observatory model, i.e. Miniland shows the two together and compresses the distance between them to nothing.
- **Lesson:** the observatory-above-sign pairing is the accepted LA shorthand. Without legs the letters read as a garden sign; we keep legs (§1).

## 3. LEGOLAND Miniland (park not stated; Miniland USA section with Statue of Liberty / Empire State nearby), photographed by Matthew Wells (2018, 2021)
URL: https://www.flickr.com/photos/coastermadmatt/51624409696/ · https://www.flickr.com/photos/coastermadmatt/30863372567/ · files `miniland-hollywood-coastermadmatt.jpg`, `miniland-hollywood-ny-coastermadmatt.jpg`
- Spells HOLLYWOOD. Letters ≈ 12 bricks tall, ≈ 5 studs wide, stroke 2 studs, **white bricks studs-up**, stud tops visible; O rounded by 1-stud steps; Y and W in stepped diagonals. No legs; on turf among real rocks.
- **Lesson:** at Miniland size the W and Y diagonals are stair-stepped bricks; legibility holds even with steps. Our letters will be smaller, so diagonals must be simplified further (§Borrow).

## 4. LEGO Ideas "Hollywood Sign", imaxedlp (2026, 496 pieces), micro/mini scale
URL (Ideas page returns 403 to curl and WebFetch): https://www.yankodesign.com/2026/04/27/someone-finally-built-the-hollywood-sign-out-of-lego-and-it-actually-slaps/ · files `ideas-imaxedlp-1.jpg` … `-3.jpg`
- **Letters:** **Light Bluish Gray**, not white, built from bricks/plates studs-forward, each ≈ 4 studs wide × 4 bricks; counters as 1×1 holes; letters set at slight angles "mirroring how the actual letters sit unevenly on the hillside".
- **Legs:** LBG bars on round plates under every letter.
- **Hill:** terraced **Tan** with slopes and wedges, many Green palm-leaf plants (palms do not grow on Mount Lee). Props: clapperboard, film camera, popcorn bucket.
- **Mast:** Dark Red lattice (support/lattice pieces) with a white/yellow top — the only build that keeps the summit antenna.
- Reads: the word, the legs, the mast. Fails: grey letters (look dirty), Tan + bright palms read "beach", props are cartoon.
- **Lesson:** keep the mast (a lattice support in Red/White); keep the uneven angles; do not copy the colours or the props.

## 5. Rebrickable MOC-25470 "Hollywood Sign", TSK15 (352 parts, part of a "midi" LA skyline)
URL: https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-25470/TSK15/hollywood-sign/ (403 to curl; image from cdn) · file `rebrickable-tsk15-moc25470.png`
- Letters are white SNOT sub-builds (brackets + tiles) set in a row along a stepped **Green/Dark Green** slope. The render shows the back; the letters are hard to read.

## Borrow / avoid (our scale: a stud is a stud, island ≈ 56–80 studs)

**Scale decision (needs Eric):** at the same scale as the observatory (Griffith 236 ft → ≈ 30 studs) the sign would be ≈ 44 studs long and taller than the observatory. Two honest options:
| | A · minifig-scale sign (Brickman) | B · background sign (BrickBlvd / how it reads from the basin) |
|---|---|---|
| Letter | 3 studs wide × 11 plates, studs-up White bricks (stroke·counter·stroke); W 4 studs | 2 studs wide × 3 studs (7–8 plates), White plates/tiles SNOT; O and D from round moulds |
| Sign length | 9 × 3 + 1 + 8 gaps of 1 ≈ **36 studs** (length/height ≈ 8.2, real 7.8) | ≈ **20–22 studs** (≈ 7.3) |
| Effect | a second hero; takes half the island width | sits on the back ridge as a backdrop, the observatory stays the hero |
| Recommend | | **B**, on the highest back ridge, with the mast — it is how the sign actually reads in the city, and Miniland/BrickBlvd both pair it this way |

**Borrow**
- **White letters, 1 stud thick, on grey legs** (LBG 1×1 round bricks or bars on round plates, 1–2 plates of visible gap, more on the downhill side) with a hint of girts/braces behind (§1, §4).
- **3-column letter grid** (stroke ≈ counter ≈ 0.23 H), letters ≈ 0.75 H wide, W ≈ 1.3 × wider, gaps ≈ 0.15 H (§0).
- **Letters step up the slope left→right** and stand at slightly uneven angles (§0, §4).
- **Dark Tan dirt + Dark Brown/Reddish Brown chaparral + Olive Green clumps**; a Tan trail along the contour in front of the letters (§0, §1).
- **Summit mast** to the right of the D: a Red/White lattice support with a few white 1×1 dishes (§4, real photos).
- Rounded O/D: real round moulds at small scale (§6); 1-stud steps at large scale (§1, §3).

**Avoid**
- Grey or cream letters (§4), green hills (§5, §7), palms on Mount Lee (§4), props (camera, popcorn).
- Letters without legs (§2, §3) — reads as a garden sign.
- Abstracting the word away (§7).

## Risks / for and against including it
- **For:** the single most recognisable LA symbol; one short white row = high recognition per part; pairs naturally with Griffith (visible from the observatory terrace, `../la-griffith/real-dome-with-sign.jpg`).
- **Against:** (1) **trademark** — the Sign's name and likeness are registered by the Hollywood Chamber of Commerce; LEGO's own park writes LEGOWOOD and there is no official set. For a personal, non-commercial site the risk is low, but flag it if the app is ever sold or promoted. (2) Scale conflict above: it must be either a second hero or a deliberately smaller backdrop. (3) It is just letters: no life-layer function of its own (a hiker minifig on the trail would be the pairing).
