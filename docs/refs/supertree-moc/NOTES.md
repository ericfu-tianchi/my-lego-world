# Supertree Grove (Gardens by the Bay) — LEGO references

Collected 2026-09-19. Part names are types, not numbers, unless the source gave one.

## Real thing (for proportions and colour)

- `real-supertree-ocbc-skyway-commons-2012.jpg` — Wikimedia Commons, Allie Caulfield, CC BY 2.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:OCBC_Skyway,_Supertree_Grove,_Gardens_by_the_Bay,_Singapore_-_20120617.jpg
- `real-supertree-grove-commons-2016.jpg` — Wikimedia Commons. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2016-04-03_Supertree_Grove,_Gardens_by_the_Bay_15.jpg
- `real-supertrees-tbb.jpg` — photo used in the Brothers Brick review. https://www.brothers-brick.com/2021/12/04/lego-architecture-21057-singapore-review/

What to copy: a **slim near-vertical trunk** (a wrapped steel lattice over a concrete core, reading dark magenta / reddish-purple with green planting), that **flares suddenly** in the top quarter into an **inverted cone / wine-glass canopy** made of thin ribs; the canopy rim is wider than the trunk by 3–4×. Canopy-to-trunk-height ratio about 1 : 3. Rib colour is dusty purple-magenta, planting is dark green with pink/red accents. The **OCBC Skyway** is a thin yellow-orange bridge slung between two of the tall trees roughly two thirds of the way up, with a handrail; it is the one horizontal line in the grove. Trees come in three heights (25 m / 35 m / 50 m) and stand in a cluster, never in a row.

## Build 1 — LEGO Architecture 21057 Singapore (official, 2022)

Files: `21057-jays-supertrees-closeup.jpg`, `21057-jays-supertrees.jpg`, `21057-jays-gbtb-1.jpg`, `21057-jays-gbtb-2.jpeg`, `21057-tbb-supertrees-43.jpg`
Sources: Jay's Brick Blog https://jaysbrickblog.com/reviews/review-lego-architecture-21057-singapore-guest-review/ ; Brothers Brick https://www.brothers-brick.com/2021/12/04/lego-architecture-21057-singapore-review/

- Size: six trees, each 1×1 footprint; heights 3–6 bricks incl. canopy. Whole grove ~6×3 studs in front of MBS.
- Trunk: stacked **1×1 round bricks** in green, some on a **1×1 round plate** in dark bluish grey, the tallest on a **2×2 cone**; a **1×1 cone** or **1×1 round brick** as the neck under the canopy.
- Canopy: **recoloured wheel hubs / rims in magenta** — "smaller hubcaps from Speed Champions cars and larger ones recoloured from the silver James Bond Aston Martin wheels" (Jay's). The spokes read as the rib lattice; the hollow centre reads as the open canopy bowl. Two large + four small hubs.
- Clinging plants: not built; the green trunk itself carries the "planted" reading.
- Skyway: not built.
- Verdict from reviewers: clever but "lacks clarity without context" (TBB). Magenta is louder than the real dusty purple.

## Build 2 — LEGOLAND Malaysia MINILAND Singapore (2025 revamp, 1:20-ish)

Files: `miniland-legoland-my-gbtb.png` (TheSmartLocal), `miniland-legoland-my-mothership.png` (Mothership)
Sources: https://thesmartlocal.com/read/legoland-malaysia-miniland-singapore/ ; https://mothership.sg/2025/08/malaysia-legoland-revamped-miniland-singapore/

- Size: minifig-scale grove, trees roughly 40–60 bricks tall.
- Trunk: a dark-green brick core clad in **green plates/tiles** (reads as the planting), wrapped with **magenta bars / flex tubes** running diagonally as the lattice.
- Canopy: an explosion of **magenta bar-and-clip / antenna-style elements** angled outward from a ring, so the silhouette is a flared bowl; interior of the bowl shows lighting elements (trans-purple / trans-blue).
- Skyway: a **yellow-orange fenced walkway** (yellow fence pieces on plates) slung between two trunks at about two-thirds height, with minifigs walking on it. This is the element that makes the grove instantly legible at minifig scale.
- Life layer: hawker stalls, benches and dozens of minifigs at the base; the trees sit on a raised lawn.

File: `../mbs-moc/moc-84745-wadjetik-mbs-artscience.png` (Supertree at left of the MBS micro model)

- Micro Supertree in white: a **1×1 round brick** trunk, a **1×1 brick with side stud** at the base, and a ring of **1×1 clip / bar holders** fanning out on a **2×2 round plate**, each holding a **1×1 brick with stud on side** — a flared crown from eight radial elements. ## Takeaways for a 56×44-stud island (landmark ≤ 0.6 × island width, minifig life in front)

1. **Two-part silhouette or nothing.** Trunk must be visibly slimmer than the canopy (1×1 or 2×2 round trunk, canopy 4–6 studs across) and the flare must start high (top quarter). A tapered cone is wrong — the real canopy is an inverted cone that opens outward.
2. **Canopy = radial ribs, not a solid disc.** 21057's hub-cap works because it has spokes and an open centre. Our equivalent with official moulds: a **2×2 round plate / 2×2 dish** ring carrying 6–8 **bars in clips** or **1×1 round plates on antennae** leaning outward; or a **wheel rim** in an official dark-purple / magenta colour if the LDraw part exists. Never a hand-invented shape (rule L3).
3. **Colour: dusty purple lattice on dark-green planting**, one accent of pink/red. 21057's bright magenta is louder than the real thing and would break the Kyoto-calibrated palette; pick the closest official purple (dark purple / magenta) and keep the ribs thin so the colour reads as line, not mass.
4. **Build the Skyway.** A thin **bright-light-orange / yellow** plate bridge with a railing between the two tallest trees at two-thirds height is the one detail that reads "Supertree Grove" instantly at minifig scale (Miniland proves it); it also gives figures somewhere to stand. Use the `railing` helper so posts are regular.
5. **Cluster of three heights, not a row.** Three trees (tall / medium / short), staggered, registered in `Sites` so they never touch each other or MBS; the tallest is the middle height tier under MBS (hero) and above the shophouses.
