**Published figures** (griffithobservatory.org "Fun Facts", via search): building **236 ft wide** (E–W, excluding the terrace ramps, +10 ft each), **176 ft deep** (N–S); lawn to top of the **main dome 77 ft**, to top of a **telescope dome 55 ft**; planetarium dome **75 ft** inside diameter. Opened 1935, Art Deco / Greek Revival, renovated and expanded underground 2002–2006 [M for dates]. Three copper-clad domes.

- **Ratios that matter:** main dome Ø ≈ **2.4 ×** a side dome; main top ≈ **1.4 ×** side top; body ≈ 0.34 × main top. The main dome is a near-hemisphere on a tall drum, **at the rear (north) of the plan**; the two telescope domes sit on cylindrical drums at the **front corners (E and W ends)**, rising out of the wing roofs. The central **octagonal roof (Hall of the Eye rotunda) sits between them, in front of the main dome**, over the entrance (`real-park-view.jpg`, `real-aerial.jpg`).
- **Plan:** a symmetrical E–W bar (two wings + central entrance pavilion) with the rotunda behind the pavilion and the planetarium drum behind that; flat **roof terraces** (walkable, buff paving) over the wings; **curved ramps/stairs wrap the outside of each telescope drum** down to the lawn; a parapet promenade runs round the rear (`real-04.jpg` shows its steps).
- **Front lawn:** on the axis, a rectangle of lawn ≈ building width, split by a central path and a cross path into **four lawn panels**, with the **Astronomers Monument on a round paved island** at the crossing; side lawns beyond. Paths are pale concrete.
- **Facade details** (`real-2011.jpg`, `real-04.jpg`): white rusticated walls with horizontal joints; Greek-key frieze; tall narrow **recessed dark-green windows** (seven per wing) between flat pilasters; the pavilion carries **GRIFFITH OBSERVATORY** in dark capitals over **bronze doors** in a stepped frame; **two Italian cypresses** flank the doors; the drum has **stepped-cap pilasters (≈ 16) rising above a scalloped frieze** and small arched windows.
- **Side domes:** a **vertical telescope shutter slit with two raised ribs** runs over the crown (clear on Miniland 2008, `miniland-ca-lightdome-greensofgrafton.jpg`, and on `real-park-view.jpg`).
- **Astronomers Monument** [M]: 1934 PWAP concrete obelisk ≈ 40 ft, six astronomers (Hipparchus, Copernicus, Galileo, Kepler, Newton, Herschel) round the base, an armillary sphere on top. White. On the photo it is about as tall as the body parapet.

**Correction to the brief:** the main dome is **copper-brown, not green**. Only the small octagonal rotunda roof is verdigris green. LEGOLAND Miniland re-domed its model from grey to Reddish Brown to match (§1). Builders who used green domes (§5) copied the old patina or guessed.

- **Domes:** studs-up, **concentric stepped rings of plates** — a stepped hemisphere whose stud rings echo the real dome's standing-seam bands. Grey (Dark Bluish Gray / LBG in sun) until ≈ 2013; **Reddish Brown by 2014** (`miniland-ca-reddome-kwschaff.jpg`) — they recoloured to match the renovated copper.
- **Side domes:** stepped rings on white drums, **with the shutter slit and its two ribs built as a separate band over the crown**.
- **Drum:** white, ≈ 16 pilasters (1×1 columns) standing above the parapet with stepped caps, arched openings, a frieze row — the most detailed part.
- **Rotunda roof:** Sand Green / Dark Turquoise flat octagon with a crest.
- **Body:** white bricks with recessed dark windows, roof terraces in Dark Red/Reddish Brown (tiles), stair ramps curling round the drums, minifigs on the terraces.
- **Site:** on a real-turf hill, the LEGOWOOD sign on the slope below (`../la-hollywood-sign/`).
- Reads well: silhouette (big dome rear, two small domes front), the pilastered drum. Weak: stepped domes look like knitted caps up close; the model is mostly seen from its north (rear) side.
- **Lesson:** the **pilastered drum** and the **slit on the side domes** are the details that make it Griffith rather than "a building with domes". Colour: Reddish Brown domes.

## Candidate moulds (checked in the vendored LDraw library; colour availability not checked — [verify] on BrickLink)
- **Main dome (≈ 10 studs):** four × `76776` Brick 5×5×3.333 Corner Round with Dome Top (→ 10×10, ≈ 10 plates rise, studs-up, true curve); or `98107` Hemisphere 11×11 with Studs on Top; or `4413` Technic Dome 11×11×5.5. `50990` Dish 10×10 inverted only for a flat cap/ring.
- **Side domes (≈ 4 studs):** `86500` Dome 4×4 Smooth; `30208` Hemisphere 4×4 Multifaceted. Shutter slit: unresolved — candidates are four `88293` 3×3×2 round-corner dome tops split by a 1-stud gap (but 6 studs = too big) or a rib of 1×N curved slopes over a smaller dome; needs a sketch.
- **Drum:** round 10×10 ring (macaroni / round-corner bricks) faced with 1×1 round bricks or grille tiles as pilasters; `3062b` Brick 1×1 Round.
- **Rotunda roof:** Sand Green `3960` Dish 4×4 / `44375` Dish 6×6 over a round plate, a ring of 1×1 plant/flower plates as the crest.
- **Monument:** White 2×2 → 1×1 round taper, cone, bar, a small gold piece on top.

## Borrow / avoid (our scale: island ≈ 56–80 studs, hero ≤ 0.6 × island width tall in plates)

**Proposed size** (W ≈ 30 studs, footprint ≈ 30 × 22 + lawn ≈ 30 × 16 in front):
| | True ratio | Proposal (heights stretched so a minifig fits a door and the facade reads) |
|---|---|---|
| Body parapet | 8 plates | **12 plates** (1 brick over minifig height) |
| Side dome | Ø 4.3 studs, top 17 plates | **Ø 4** (`86500`), drum top ≈ 15, dome top ≈ 20 plates |
| Main dome | Ø 10.4 studs, top 24 plates | **Ø 10** (4 × `76776`), drum top ≈ 20, dome top ≈ 30 plates |
| Main top / side top | 1.4 | 1.5 |
- 30 plates is well under 0.6 × island width (≈ 38 plates at 64 studs), so Griffith can be the hero with headroom; or the Hollywood Sign ridge behind can be higher.

**Borrow**
- **Reddish Brown copper domes** (Miniland's current choice) and **one Sand Green octagonal rotunda roof** in front of the main dome.
- **True-curve dome moulds**, not stepped plates (§3, §4 vs §1, §2).
- **Pilastered drum** (≈ 16 pilasters above the parapet) under the main dome (§1, §4, §5).
- **Side-dome shutter slit with ribs** (§1, §5) — the detail most builds skip.
- **Facade rhythm:** seven tall dark windows per wing between pilasters, the central pavilion with the name band and dark doors, **two Italian cypresses** at the door (§4, `real-2011.jpg`). The name band is an L2 print candidate (carved capitals, dark on white).
- **Front lawn in four panels on a cross of pale paths with the white Astronomers Monument on a round island** (§3, §5, real aerials) — the strongest top-down read and a natural place for the paired minifig (a visitor at the telescope / a stargazer).
- **Roof terraces in Tan**, curved ramps round the telescope drums (§1, §2).
- **Olive Green / Dark Green chaparral hill with conifers** under the building (§5); the observatory on a spur below the Hollywood Sign ridge (§1, §6, `real-dome-with-sign.jpg`).

**Avoid**
- Green or black main domes (§4, §5); grey domes (pre-2006 look).
- Stepped-plate domes at ≈ 10 studs (§2).
- Side domes larger than ≈ 0.45 × the main dome (§2).

## For / against including it
- **For:** strongest building silhouette in LA (one big dome + two small, symmetric); white body + brown domes is a clean, calm palette that fits the series; the lawn/monument gives the open-ground share the rules ask for; real dome moulds exist at exactly the right sizes (4 and 10 studs); pairs with the Hollywood Sign in the true sightline.
- **Against:** none structural. Watch-outs: the slit on 4-stud domes has no obvious mould solution yet; the main dome mould choice (`76776` ×4 vs `98107`) must be checked for colour existence and for how its studs read; no official set to copy, so the build scheme is ours (Miniland is the only large-scale reference and it is mostly photographed from the rear).
