# Prague in LEGO: how other builders did it (research notes, 2026-10-02)

Scope: official sets, the Prague LEGO museum, fan MOCs (Rebrickable / Flickr / Brickset / Brothers Brick / Eurobricks / LEGO Ideas), Miniland, LEGO Masters.
Confidence tags: **[seen]** = I looked at the photo myself; **[src]** = stated by the source; **[mine]** = my reading / LEGO knowledge, verify against LDraw before baking.
Access limits: LEGO Ideas and Rebrickable return 403/bot-challenge to scripted fetches, so for those only search snippets were available (no photos, no inventories).

## 0. Bottom line: official LEGO for Prague

- **No official LEGO set of any Prague landmark exists** (no Architecture Prague / skyline, no Icons, no City set). The Architecture list covers Paris, London, Venice, Berlin, Tokyo, etc., but not Prague. [src] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego_Architecture
- Closest was **LEGO Ideas "Dancing House, Prague" by paranoider (Yuan Qi)**: 2,987 pcs, 10k supporters on 2024-03-03, **rejected in review**. [src] https://www.brickfanatics.com/public/index.php/lego-ideas-inspired-unusual-building-10k-mark , https://ideas.lego.com/projects/84c42be3-cad7-49db-b2bc-4d0bf6db490c
- Other Ideas proposals (none reached 10k): "Prague – LEGO Architecture" (bridge with baroque statues, expired 2021-11-09) https://ideas.lego.com/projects/ac4a637d-8432-4e07-8e78-55ee20f55837 ; "Prague – Charles Bridge" https://ideas.lego.com/projects/b01246b2-5b63-4ef2-b8fc-3560a351efbc ; "Prague's Charles Bridge" (with tower) https://beta.ideas.lego.com/product-ideas/0a4a0711-5479-4304-a29b-fde547bcd4ea ; two Astronomical Clock proposals (below); a historic Prague sightseeing tram at minifig scale https://ideas.lego.com/projects/8fd74732-441d-4458-abb4-919689a4804b ; Tatra T3 trams https://ideas.lego.com/projects/e4b5599f-2ab2-45a3-958a-2450ecad4af9 , https://ideas.lego.com/projects/654f0703-f971-4b5c-906b-1e053a1382ac
- The many "Prague Astronomical Clock 2,441 pcs" kits online (Pantasy etc.) are **clone-brand, not LEGO**. Not a reference for real moulds.
- **Miniland**: no Prague found. LEGOLAND Deutschland's 2026 Miniland press kit lists Berlin, Venice, Frankfurt, High Five skyscrapers, Neuschwanstein, Switzerland, Hamburg; no Czech area. [src] https://www.legoland.de/media/fjtezq5c/04-legoland-deutschland-miniland-2026-english.pdf . No Prague in Billund either, as far as searches show.
- **LEGO Masters**: no Prague episode found. Czech LEGO Masters (TV Nova, 2022) exists; its winner **Michal Horáček** is the key Prague builder (below). https://www.mediaguru.cz/clanky/2022/05/nova-od-cervna-nasadi-do-prime-time-show-lego-masters/

## 1. Official sets whose techniques transfer

| Set | What to borrow | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **10253 Big Ben** (2016) | **Clock face = printed 6×6 dish mounted *backwards* (concave face out) so the numerals read**, held free-floating in a frame by part geometry and a couple of rails, no stud. Hands = minifig wrenches on a central axle (they don't pass over each other cleanly). Gilded finials = gold ski poles; gothic cresting = 52 × 1×1 clips; gold flowers texture the roof base. Gold 1×1 round plates (with hole) in the corner spandrels. [src][seen] | https://brickset.com/article/22055 , https://thebrickblogger.com/2016/07/brick-breakdown-lego-creator-big-ben/ ; image `official-10253-bigben-clockface.jpg` |
| **21061 Notre-Dame** (2024, Architecture scale) | Gothic arcade from **Brick Arch 1×3×3 Gothic (13965)**, **Arch 1×2 jumper (38583)** and **Arch 1½×1½ corner (38585)** (an arch rhythm with one module between). **Rose window = Dish 4×4 trans-clear printed**. **Flying buttresses = candlesticks (upper) + droid arm (lower), bar holders with hole + bar handles.** Tower parapets = Fence 1×4×1. **Colour: Tan, not grey** (reviewer: grey sandstone MOCs are a common mistake). [src] | https://newelementary.com/2024/05/lego-architecture-review-21061-notre.html , https://brickset.com/article/109391 |
| **10332 Medieval Town Square** (2024, 3,304 pcs) | Half-timber via the **new Door 1×4×6 "wood structure" frame (5466)** that takes Window 1×2×2; latticed windows 1×2×3; Plate 2×3 with rocks (49×) for stone texture; hinged building halves; turret roof from Lion Knights' Castle. Colour pairs used: bright red+black; sand yellow+olive green. [src] | https://www.newelementary.com/2024/05/lego-icons-review-10332-medieval-town.html?m=0 , https://brickarchitect.com/?p=19816 |
| **10350 Tudor Corner** (2025) | Jettied upper floors, half-timber top floor over brick middle floor, angled corner. Based on Chester's Coach House Inn. [src] | https://www.brickfanatics.com/real-life-lego-icons-10350-tudor-corner/ |
| **10297 Boutique Hotel / 10312 Jazz Club / 10278 Police Station** | Narrow 16-wide gabled townhouse facades, cornices from inverted slopes / brackets, window surrounds from tiles and 1×1 slopes [mine: standard modular grammar; no Prague-specific review fetched]. |  |
| **10305 Lion Knights' Castle** | Turret roofs with rounded battlements (reused in 10332). [src via 10332 review] |  |
| **60423 Downtown Streetcar and Station** (2024, 811 pcs) | **First LEGO City standalone tram**: 3 articulated red cars, fixed wheel sets per car (hence the large inter-car gap; reviewers suggest shared bogies), pantograph, identical head/tail cabs, curved windscreen. 6 minifigs. [src][seen] | https://eurobricks.com/forum/forums/topic/199697-review-60423-downtown-streetcar-and-station ; image `official-60423.jpg` |
| **60506 Classic Beach Tram** (2026, 693 pcs) | Traditional-style tram: entry steps, vertical grab poles, bench seats. Closest official look to a historic Prague tram. [src] | https://www.mediamarkt.com.tr/tr/product/_lego-city-klasik-plaj-tramvayi-60506-177097524.html |
| **10308 Holiday Main Street** | Small open tram, 4 seats. [src] |  |

## 2. Muzeum LEGA / Museum of Bricks, Prague

- Národní 31, Prague 1. Opened 2011 by collector Miloš Křeček; Prague branch 420 m², ~3,000 models, >1 M bricks. Prague models: **Charles Bridge** (long billed as "the biggest LEGO model in the world"), **Old Town Hall Tower & Astronomical Clock**, **National Museum**, **National Theatre**, **Powder Tower**; also St Vitus (~400k bricks, per one tourism source; likely the Horáček model, unconfirmed). [src] https://museumofbricks.cz/lego-museum/prague , https://brickset.com/article/29468 , https://www.barcelo.com/guia-turismo/en/czech-republic/praga/things-to-do/lego-museum/
- **Old Town Hall Tower + Orloj model: 9,800 bricks, 300+ hours** (museum label) [seen, `museum-brickset-1.jpg`, Brickset, CC BY 2.0]. What the photo shows:
  - Minifig-scale tower; walls in **tan with scattered dark-tan bricks** (stone mottling), not grey.
  - Tower crown: **dark bluish grey hipped roof with four corner turrets (bartizans) on a dark-grey gallery cornice**; small clock dial high on the tower face.
  - Orloj: **two discs stacked vertically** (astrolabe above, calendar below), dark frame with gold detail; **sand-green pyramid canopy** over the clock and a sand-green gable roof with a spire above; flanking narrow vertical pilasters in dark grey.
  - Neighbouring Old Town Square houses as flat 1-module facades (white, dark red with gold gable, pink/white), arcaded ground floors with arch bricks.
- Second photo `museum-brickset-2.jpg`: a row of Prague-style townhouses (gables, arched windows, pastel colours: lavender, yellow, olive, white). Good reference for the square's house row.
- Museum photos online are mostly visitor shots; the museum's own site has no Prague-model photos.

## 3. MOCs by landmark

### 3.1 Astronomical Clock / Old Town Hall
1. **Muzeum LEGA model** (9,800 pcs, minifig scale). See §2. Best single reference for our scale. [seen]
2. **Michal Horáček, medieval village (2024)**: central red town hall + white stone tower + orloj-like clock, "heavily inspired by Prague's Old Town Hall". Stone facades heavily greebled (1×1 / 1×2 tiles, plates, brackets at random depths in light/dark bluish grey), black hip roof with flared eaves, pinnacles, half-timber neighbours. Clock: **round blue/yellow dial ~4–6 studs** under a pointed arch. [src][seen] https://www.brothers-brick.com/2024/11/08/lego-master-michal-horaceks-medieval-village-is-a-towering-teetering-triumph/ ; images `horacek-townhall-1.jpg`, `horacek-townhall-2.jpg`
3. **Pingubricks, MOC-50171 "Prague Astronomical Clock Tower (Architecture style)"**: 647 pcs, 30 cm tall, 12×14-stud base. Rebrickable blocked fetch; no technique detail. https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-50171/Pingubricks/prague-astronomical-clock-tower-architecture-style/
4. **LEGO Ideas, functional Orloj** (5 yrs, ~3,000 pcs): clock as a furnished house; handle turns hands via internal gears; apostles rotate through front openings; removable tower. [src] https://beta.ideas.lego.com/product-ideas/57201690-9d24-45ac-ba5b-24ede056921d
5. **LEGO Ideas "medieval spaceship" Orloj**: static calendar plate below, rotating astrolabe face, apostle windows above, driven by two side cogs, cobblestone base, transparent back. [src] https://ideas.lego.com/projects/5b9a9dcd-0e40-42ca-a5c5-fb26b56188cb
6. **Jet Kwan micro skyline (2020)**: Old Town Hall tower in tan with dark-grey roof + tiny round dial. [seen] `jetkwan-prague-1.jpg`

### 3.2 Charles Bridge + Old Town Bridge Tower
1. **Muzeum LEGA Charles Bridge**: the museum's flagship; no technique write-up or usable photo found.
2. **Schneider Cheung ("Schfio factory"), Hong Kong 2011 "Travel around the world" show**: minifig scale, **96 × 192 studs**, built in 1.5 weeks, SNOT throughout, bridge set at an angle to the scene, street lamps, a tram. Photo shows the **bridge walls in dark bluish grey + tan mixed bricks, segmental arches over blue water, round minifig-scale lamps, small trees; towers in tan/dark tan with dark grey pointed roofs and corner pinnacles**. [src][seen] https://www.flickr.com/photos/schfio/albums/72157626386935115/ , https://www.eurobricks.com/forum/forums/topic/54309-my-lego-st-basils-cathedral-and-the-charles-bridge-of-prague/ ; image `schfio-charles-bridge-0018.jpg`
3. **Jet Kwan micro**: **1×4 arches** in light bluish grey on piers with **wedge/cheese slopes as cutwaters** pointing upstream in the water; tan tile deck coping; **statues = grey minifig statuettes (trophy figure) on 1×1 plinths** at each pier; bridge tower in LBG with tan and black, dark-grey pyramid roof, black corner pinnacles. [src][seen] https://www.brothers-brick.com/2020/08/06/prague-only-smaller/ ; `jetkwan-prague-4.jpg`
4. **djmouka, MOC-148868 "Prague Skyline"** (430 pcs, includes bridge + Dancing House). https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-148868/djmouka/prague-skyline/
5. **DocSpoodle, MOC-263915 "Prague Diorama"** (1,298 pcs: Charles Bridge, Lennon Wall, Kafka Museum, Dancing House, Castle, St Vitus, Týn, Orloj, around the Vltava). https://rebrickable.com/mocs/MOC-263915/DocSpoodle/prague-diorama/
6. LEGO Ideas proposals listed in §0.

### 3.3 Týn Church
- Only micro builds found. **Jet Kwan**: the multi-spire crown = **black telescope elements + palm-tree tops + 1×1 cones**; towers tan; nave roof orange; front houses as tan 1×1 arch-topped gables. [src][seen] `jetkwan-prague-2.jpg`
- DocSpoodle's diorama includes Týn (no detail).
- No minifig-scale Týn MOC found. **Gap: we will be designing this one.**
- Transferable gothic-spire vocabulary from §1: 21061 (gothic arch 1×3×3, tan), Big Ben (ski-pole finials, 1×1 clips cresting), Horáček (pinnacles on corner turrets).

### 3.4 St Vitus Cathedral
- **Michal Horáček, 2015: 350,000 bricks, 5 m × 2 m, 1,800 h**, built by eye from book plans and photos, no CAD; shown ~2 years, then dismantled. [src] https://cc.cz/lego-do-trezoru-nepatri-rika-znamy-stavitel-z-kostek-buduje-obri-katedraly-i-svet-kingdom-come/ (photos in the e15 gallery, CDN blocks download: https://www.e15.cz/galerie/archiv/229060/lego-pro-pokrocile-funkcni-bugatti-psaci-stroj-i-zachod)
- Jet Kwan micro: dark tan + black + sand-green spires, black twin west towers. [seen] `jetkwan-prague-1.jpg`
- Schfio scene and DocSpoodle diorama include the Castle and Cathedral.

### 3.5 Dancing House
1. **paranoider / Yuan Qi, LEGO Ideas (2,987 pcs, 10k, not approved)**. What the renders show [seen]: **"Ginger" glass tower = trans-light-blue plates (2×4, 2×6 etc.) on hinge plates in a faceted cone pinched at the waist**, standing on splayed **light-grey sloped piloti (inverted slopes and wedges)**; **"Fred" = stacked white curved slope/bow rings with bay windows deliberately offset per floor; the wavy façade lines = thin dark-grey tile/plate strips in a stepped wave**; **"Medusa" dome = a dark-grey / trans-black faceted sphere** on a salmon-red rooftop; round grey 2×2 brick columns at street level. https://cc.cz/tancici-dum-jako-stavebnice-lego-i-diky-vam-muze-slavny-kousek-prahy-vyjit-v-kostickove-podobe/ ; images `paranoider-dancinghouse-01/03/05.jpg`
2. **Jet Kwan micro**: mostly 1×1 slopes, tiles and bricks. [src] `jetkwan-prague-1.jpg`
3. djmouka MOC-148868 and DocSpoodle MOC-263915 (Rebrickable, no detail).

### 3.6 Prague tram
- Official: **60423** (red modern, articulated) and **60506** (classic) are the only official minifig-scale trams with a real tram body (§1).
- **Darkkostas25 (Eurobricks), Tatra T3: 6 studs wide, bogies 4 studs**, Studio file https://www.bricklink.com/v3/studio/design.page?idModel=314099 . (His is the Ukrainian green/yellow livery; Prague's T3 is **red and cream**.) https://www.eurobricks.com/forum/forums/topic/191786-moc-trams/
- LEGO Ideas historic Prague sightseeing tram at minifig scale, 6 figures, driver in historic uniform. [src] https://ideas.lego.com/projects/8fd74732-441d-4458-abb4-919689a4804b
- Tatra T3/T6A5 Powered Up MOC, 84 cm long (much larger than minifig scale). [src] Ideas project e4b5599f.
- Schfio's 2011 Charles Bridge scene also had a tram.

### 3.7 Other Prague buildings (style references)
- **poMOCník & dirigent, Municipal House / Insurance building (Art Nouveau)**: tan Bionicle / Robo Rider parts for organic curves, gold + green for patinated metalwork. https://www.brothers-brick.com/2024/09/30/czech-out-this-art-nouveau-modular-of-a-prague-landmark/ ; `pomocnik-municipal-insurance.jpg`
- Łukasz Libuszewski, Old Town Pub (Prague-inspired modular). https://www.brothers-brick.com/2017/10/10/buying-round-may-render-penniless/
