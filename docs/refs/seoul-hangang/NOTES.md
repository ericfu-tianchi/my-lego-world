# Seoul candidates (Han River, Lotte World Tower, DDP, Cheonggyecheon, Gwanghwamun): reference notes
2026-10-06 · research only, no code · images in this folder · [M] = from memory, not verified in a source · part numbers verified against the first line of the LDraw `.dat` in `vendor/ldraw-lib/ldraw/parts`

Question: which of five Seoul subjects earn a place on a 4–5-place island (rules 5, 6, 13, 14; playbook "a river or a sea gets traffic"), and how each would be built. Shanghai is already on the globe with a river through the middle, a pale tapering supertall (Shanghai Tower) and a white sightseeing boat, so every echo of it is called out.

## Verdicts at a glance
| | Subject | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Han River + Banpo Bridge (+ riverside park life) | **IN: the calm centre and the life layer.** Fountain: **trial one bay, cut it if it reads as a comb** | Seoul's defining open space. The life (chimaek on the lawn, cycle path, delivery zone, ramyeon) is unique to Seoul and carries the figures. The fountain is a night show; by day it is white spray, which bricks can only hint at |
| B | Lotte World Tower | **IN: the hero** | The skyline's single dominant object, and every brick version agrees on its identifier (taper + seam + split crown). Must be built **White, not glass-blue**, to stop it reading as a second Shanghai Tower |
| C | DDP | **OUT** (reserve) | No brick version found to copy. A doubly-curved, low, 280 m silver blob compresses to a grey lump at our scale |
| D | Cheonggyecheon | **OUT** | A second waterway beside the Han, and at our scale a sunken ditch. Its one signature, *Spring*, is a 20 m Oldenburg spiral that is hard to build and is a copyrighted artwork |
| E | Gwanghwamun Square | **IN, but anchored on the Gwanghwamun gate + Bugaksan, with the gold seated Sejong in front.** Admiral Yi is optional | The square alone is flat paving with two statues. Gate + mountain + king is how everyone photographs it. The statues need brick-sculpting, as Miniland does it |

Suggested 4–5 places: **Han River with a Banpo bridge segment** (centre), **Lotte World Tower** (south-east bank, Jamsil), **Gwanghwamun gate + Sejong** (north bank). The 4th/5th could be N Seoul Tower on Namsan (in every skyline MOC, not in this brief), or a hanok lane. Eric decides.

### 0.1 Han River at Banpo (A)
- **Banpo Bridge** (Wikipedia):
  - 1,495 m long, 25 m wide, completed 1982.
  - It is Korea's first double-deck bridge: it sits **on top of Jamsu Bridge** (795 m × 18 m), which floods in high water.
- **Structure** (`real-banpo-bridge.jpg`):
  - a pale-grey concrete box deck on **paired round-cornered rectangular columns** joined by a crossbeam, spans ≈ 2.5–3 × the deck height above the water [measured];
  - **Jamsu runs low between the piers**, a few metres above the water, with a light steel pipe railing; pedestrians and cyclists use it.
  - The upper deck has a thin parapet with closely spaced lamp posts.
- **Moonlight Rainbow Fountain** (Wikipedia, Seocho-gu, Seoul Hangang site):
  - installed 2009, Guinness "longest bridge fountain" 2008;
  - **380 nozzles** along 1,140 m of both sides, 190 t of river water a minute, deck ≈ 20 m above the water, jets reach ≈ 43 m sideways [thisiskoreatours];
  - runs April–October, 20-minute shows several times a day, synced to music.
- **What it looks like:**
  - **By day:** a fan of **white water arcs leaving the deck edge and dropping into the river** well out from the bridge, read as mist and spray (`real-banpo-fountain-day.jpg` at dusk).
  - **At night:** the same arcs **lit in rainbow LED colour**. The "rainbow" exists only at night, and our sets run daytime (Shanghai's night mode is off).
- **Some Sevit** beside Banpo: three floating glass pavilions, plus round pedal boats with orange sunshades (`real-sevit-boat.jpg`).
- **River colour** (photos): olive-khaki-green in sun, grey-blue under cloud. This is **close to Shanghai's murky jade**, so it needs a slightly more olive mix to tell the two apart.
- **Riverside park (the life):**
  - **chimaek**: fried chicken + beer delivered to the riverbank. Seoul built "Delivery Zones" in 2016 (three at Yeouido) where riders hand over the boxes.
  - People sit on **picnic mats packed along the river rail** (`real-hangang-yeouido.jpg`). Mats are bought at the CU/GS25 convenience stores, which also sell ramyeon cooked on an instant-ramyeon machine [M; widely reported, no source opened].
  - Small sun tents ≤ 2 × 2 m are allowed in 14 zones.
  - The **cycle path** is dark asphalt with a **yellow centre line and light-blue edges** (`real-hangang-bikepath.jpg`); the green-and-white Ddareungi shared bikes are on it [M].
- **Traffic on the river:**
  - **Hangang Bus**: launched 18 Sep 2025 (suspended for ten days, resumed 1 Nov). Catamarans **35 m × 9.5 m**, 199 seats, **white with a blue gradient**, 12 boats, 7 piers from Magok to Jamsil (Wikipedia, Korea Times). This is the 2026-current river boat and replaces the old water taxi.
  - Also E-Land cruise boats and jet-skis (`real-banpo-fountain-b.jpg`).
  - Swan/duck pedal boats exist (Ttukseom) but are minor. **There is no swan mould in the LDraw library** (grep "swan": none).

### 0.2 Lotte World Tower (B)
- **Facts** (Wikipedia, KPF, skyscraper.org):
  - 555 m, 123 floors, KPF, opened 2017, the tallest in Korea.
  - KPF: **"a sleek, tapered form with a vertical seam that splays at the top"**, drawing on Korean ceramics, porcelain and calligraphy. **One seam** tracks the full height and points towards the historic centre (north-west).
  - It tapers in both directions, "football, not baseball". The façade is **"light silver glass and white lacquered metal"**.
  - **Crown**: a diagrid steel lantern on floors 107–123, ≈ 120 m. It is two prongs either side of an open slot. The Seoul Sky observatory is in the top floors.
  - It stands on the **Lotte World Mall** podium (a white, gently curved, low mall; `real-lwt-d.jpg` bottom) in Jamsil, by Seokchon Lake. The Jamsil end of the Han is ≈ 1 km north.
- **Measured** (`real-lwt-b.jpg`, low perspective, and `real-lwt-d.jpg`):
  - the **profile is convex**: near-vertical at the bottom, curving inward faster toward the top;
  - top-of-shaft width ≈ **0.5 × base width**;
  - **crown ≈ 22 % of height**, two prongs separated by a slot ≈ ⅓ of the crown width;
  - visible H : W ≈ 6–7 : 1. Base ≈ 70 m square-ish [M].
  - Plan is a square with softened corners [M; photos agree].
- **Colours:**
  - by day the glass reads **pale silver-blue/white** (`real-lwt-c.jpg`), with **white mullion lines** and floor bands;
  - the seam reads as a **darker vertical line**;
  - the crown is the same pale glass, with lit blue diagrid at night.

### 0.3 DDP (C)
- **Facts:** Zaha Hadid; 2009–2014. It is **280 m × 188 m, 29–34 m high**, with 86,574 m² of floor area. The skin is **45,133 aluminium panels** (≈ 14,000 flat, ≈ 31,000 curved), some perforated and lit. A **walkable lawn roof** sweeps down to the skin. Beside it are the Dongdaemun History & Culture Park, excavated fortress wall, and the LED rose garden (Wikipedia, Samsung C&T, worldconstructionnetwork).
- **Look** (`real-ddp-*.jpg`):
  - long, **low, pillow-edged forms** with no corners;
  - silver-grey panel grid; a deep overhanging canopy over a sunken plaza;
  - a grass roof meeting the metal.
- **At minifig scale** (1 stud ≈ 0.33 m) DDP would be ≈ 850 × 570 studs. Compressed to ≈ 20 × 14 studs × 8–10 plates it keeps only "a grey rounded slab with a lawn on top".

### 0.4 Cheonggyecheon (D)
- **Facts:**
  - a restored urban stream, opened 2005, after the elevated highway over it was removed; 10.9 km in all;
  - it starts at **Cheonggye Plaza** with a 4 m two-tier waterfall and *Spring*;
  - *Spring*: Claes Oldenburg & Coosje van Bruggen, 2006, a **20 m red-and-blue spiral "seashell" cone**.
- **Look** (`real-cheonggye-a.jpg`):
  - a channel sunk ≈ 5 m below street level [estimate], with **granite-block walls**, a paved footpath on one side and riprap boulders on the other;
  - a narrow stream ≈ ⅓ of the channel width;
  - **paired stepping stones** across it, willows and reeds; street rails above.

### 0.5 Gwanghwamun Square (E)
- **Facts** (Wikipedia):
  - **King Sejong statue**: 2009, seated on a throne, **6.2 m tall × 4.3 m wide, on a 4.2 m granite pedestal**, gilt bronze, facing south.
  - **Admiral Yi statue**: 1968, **6.5 m figure on a 10.5 m pillar (17 m in all)**, dark bronze, sword in hand, a turtle-ship model and war drums at the base. Floor fountains play in front.
  - The two stand ≈ 250 m apart.
  - Behind them: the **Gwanghwamun gate** (a stone base with three arches and a two-tier tiled hip-and-gable roof) and **Bugaksan** mountain.
- **Look** (`real-sejong.jpg`, `real-yi-sunsin.jpg`):
  - Sejong reads as **one gold seated mass on a carved gold throne**, right hand out, a book in the left;
  - Yi reads as a **dark standing silhouette on a tall pale plinth**.

## 1. Han River / Banpo / riverside: brick versions
Brick versions of **the Han** exist only as small parts of skylines and maps. **No Banpo bridge and no rainbow fountain** in bricks was found on Rebrickable, Flickr, LEGO Ideas, The Brothers Brick, or in Korean search.
1. **Maxtud "Micro Seoul"**, Rebrickable MOC-75983 (957 pcs, free instructions) · `moc-micro-seoul-maxtud.jpg`
   - The Han as **a broad band of open water winding between green banks**, with 15 landmarks on micro bases either side.
   - **Lesson:** the river is a wide calm band that the city sits along, not a line between two skylines.
2. **Skyline_Lover "Seoul Skyline"**, MOC-200548 (716 pcs; 29 × 9 × 30 cm) · `moc-seoul-skyline-skylinelover.png`
   - The Han reduced to a strip of trans tiles in the base.
   - **Lesson:** at skyline scale the river is a token; we have room to do better.
3. **briyzexo "South Korea's Seoul Postcard"**, MOC-188782 · `moc-seoul-postcard-briyzexo.jpg`
   - The builder's text calls the Han the element "that splits the capital in two, with activities on both sides". The build is a façade postcard.
   - **Lesson:** "activities on both sides" is how a builder summarises Seoul's river.
4. **LEGO Ideas "Lego Architecture Korea"** (ideas.lego.com/projects/14ae1333-…; 403, so not opened): from the search snippet, the Han with **the Han-gang bridge** beside the 63 Building, Lotte Tower and Namsan Tower.
5. **LEGOLAND Korea Miniland** (Chuncheon, 2022; 7 M bricks in Miniland):
   - The Seoul section lists the 63 Building (on the Han bank), Lotte World Tower, N Seoul Tower, Gwanghwamun Plaza, Gyeongbokgung and others.
   - The **Busan section has ships floating offshore and the Busan bridge** (newswire, headout). No Han photo was obtained.
   - **Lesson:** Miniland treats water as a stage for boats.
6. **Official water-jet vocabulary** [M, set numbers not checked]: Friends and City park/fountain sets use **trans-clear plume and flame elements** as spray. Shanghai's own river technique is in `docs/countries/china.md` §river.

**Technique for the signature details (our scale):**
- **Bridge:**
  - a pale **Light Bluish Gray** deck **4 studs wide** (the real 25 m compresses), ≈ 12 plates above the water;
  - **paired piers** of 2 × 2 round-corner / plain bricks joined by a 2 × N plate crossbeam;
  - a low **Jamsu deck** 3 plates above the water between the piers, with a **Fence 1 × 4 × 1 `3633`** / **spindled fence `15332`** railing;
  - lamp posts on the upper parapet (bar + 1 × 1 round plate).
  - A segment crossing a ≈ 14–16-stud river means ≈ 4 spans.
- **Fountain (daylight)** · honest judgement:
  - The only bendable water moulds in LDraw are flexible hoses (`14301` Hose Flexible 11.5L, `27328`), which bake **straight**, so our pipeline cannot bend them.
  - Pre-curved candidates are **Flame 5L Curved with Bar 0.5L `18395`** and **Minifig Plume/Flame Triple `64647`**, both produced in trans colours [M]. They would be clipped by their bar into **Tile 1 × 1 with Clip `15712`** under the deck edge, arcing out and down ≈ 4 studs.
  - The renderer draws trans at 0.62 opacity, so the arcs read **frosted white**, which suits spray.
  - Risk: a row of 12–16 identical curved spikes can read as a comb or icicles. **Build one bay in the test scene and judge it beside `real-banpo-fountain-day.jpg` before committing.** No rainbow colours by day; colour is a night-mode-only idea.
- **River:**
  - trans-clear tiles over a **more olive base than Shanghai's** (e.g. Olive Green + Dark Bluish Gray rows under Trans-Clear, swatch-tested beside Shanghai's Sand Green mix);
  - a **stepped granite bank** (Light Bluish Gray plates) to the park.
- **Boat (playbook rule):** the **Hangang Bus**.
  - A catamaran ≈ 16 × 5 studs: two White hull pontoons (curved slopes `11477` / `50950` bows) under a White deck.
  - A low cabin with a band of trans-black windows, and a **Medium Azure → Dark Azure gradient** in whole plate rows on the hull sides; a wake of white tiles.
  - It is new, Seoul-specific, and differs from Shanghai's sightseeing boat (catamaran; blue gradient, not red/white).
- **Riverside life (one or two of these become the figures):**
  - a **chimaek mat**: two figures on a 4 × 4 tile mat holding **Minifig Food Turkey Leg `33057`** (the drumstick), a 2 × 2 chicken box (an L2 print with a short generic word, e.g. 치킨), beer **Minifig Bottle `95228`**;
  - a **delivery rider** on **Minifig Scooter Body `15396`** at a printed "배달존" sign;
  - **ramyeon** in **Minifig Cup `3899`** / **Minifig Rice Bowl `34172`** outside a convenience-store kiosk;
  - a **cyclist** on **Minifig Bicycle Frame `4719`** with **Wheel `64248`** on the yellow-lined path;
  - willows by the water.

**Footprint/parts:**
| Element | Footprint (studs) | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| River band | ≈ 14–16 wide across the island | ≈ 250 (tiles) |
| Bridge segment | 18–20 × 4 | ≈ 250–300 |
| Fountain trial | — | ≈ 30 |
| Hangang Bus | 16 × 5 | ≈ 150 |
| Park strip, path and picnic | 20 × 8 | ≈ 250 |
| **Total** | | **≈ 900–1,000** |

**Echo of Shanghai and how to avoid it:**
- Shanghai = a river **front to back** with **a skyline on both banks**.
- Seoul = the river **across** the island, **crossed by the double-deck bridge**, with **park life on the bank** (lawn, cycle path, picnic) instead of skyline facing skyline.
- Use a different boat and a more olive water.

## 2. Lotte World Tower: brick versions
1. **FunnyTacoBunny "Lotte World Tower at 1/650"**, MOC-49690 (13,954 pcs) · `moc-lwt-funnytacobunny-v1.png`
   - Dark blue-glass shaft with **stepped setbacks** near the top and a rounded light-blue cap.
   - **Lesson (negative):** discrete setbacks read Art Deco; the cap without the slot loses the identity.
2. **FunnyTacoBunny "LWT Ver 2 at 1/650"**, MOC-143208 (10,331 pcs) · `moc-lwt-funnytacobunny-v2.jpg`
   - A **trans-clear skin over a dark core**, a continuous convex taper, a **split crown with an open slot**, and a white podium with a lawn.
   - **Lesson:** the slot and the continuous taper are the identity. The author's own v1 → v2 fix was exactly these.
3. **leblegun "Lotte World Tower"**, MOC-187077 (1,509 pcs, paid PDF) · `moc-lwt-leblegun.png`
   - **All White**: four stacked sections with curved-slope shoulders and rounded corners, a tall slotted spire, dark window tiles.
   - **Lesson:** White reads as "porcelain" (KPF's own story) and keeps it clear of glass-blue towers. But the curved shoulders at each section read like bamboo joints, so taper continuously instead.
4. **bricks_with_sunny "Seoul Skyline"**, MOC-222658 (611 pcs) · `moc-seoul-skyline-sunny.png`
   - Medium Azure stacked curved slopes with a **grey seam running up the centre into the split top**.
   - **Lesson:** a contrasting seam line is legible even at micro scale.
5. **Skyline_Lover "Seoul Skyline"**, MOC-200548 · `moc-seoul-skyline-skylinelover.png`
   - Bright Light Blue stepped tower with a grey slotted crown.
   - **Lesson:** the slotted crown again.
6. **studsandtubes "Seoul Skyline"**, MOC-58707 (680 pcs) · `moc-seoul-skyline-studsandtubes.jpg`
   - Dark grey shaft, **two prongs with a slot** on top.
7. **Maxtud "Micro Seoul"**, MOC-75983 · `moc-micro-seoul-maxtud.jpg`
   - A white micro tower with tiers and a pointed top.
8. **LEGO Store Jamsil LWT + Mall diorama** (LEGO Korea, 2024, **≈ 3 m tall, designed at nanofig scale**, in the store at Lotte World Mall B1; Seoul Economic Daily, YouTube "레고로 만든 3m 대형 롯데월드몰&롯데월드타워"). No still saved.
9. **LEGOLAND Korea Miniland** includes LWT (headout listing). No photo obtained.
10. **LEGO Ideas "South Korea, Seoul"** skyline (241 × 280 mm) includes LWT (403, not opened).

**Consensus:** all versions keep **the taper and the slotted two-prong crown**; the better ones add **the seam**.

**Technique for our scale (borrow):**
- **Footprint:** a 6 × 6 shaft with **rounded corners** (Brick 1 × 1 Round `3062b` / Tile 1 × 1 Corner Round `25269` at the corners).
- **Height:** ≈ **96–104 plates** (≈ 6.5 : 1, a pair with Shanghai's 6–7 : 1 rule), so it is the tallest object on the island and still ≤ the cap.
- **Taper:**
  - **SNOT cladding** on **Brick 1 × 1 with Stud on 1 Side `87087`** / **Brick 1 × 2 with Two Studs on One Side `11211`**;
  - each face steps in by **one plate thickness** (0.4 stud) every ≈ 10–12 plates, with setbacks **closer together toward the top**, so the profile reads convex rather than stepped.
  - Six or seven setbacks take the 6-stud base to ≈ 3 studs under the crown.
- **Skin:** **White** 1 × 2 / 1 × 4 tiles with **Light Bluish Gray Tile 1 × 2 Grille `2412b`** floor bands every few rows.
  - Opaque, not trans: the renderer turns trans towers to frost, and Shanghai already owns pale-blue glass.
  - Alternative to test: White frame + **Light Aqua** tile panels, which reads "silver-blue glass" without trans.
- **Seam:** a **1-plate-deep, ½-stud-wide recessed channel** in Dark Bluish Gray up the centre of the north-west face, full height, opening into the crown slot.
- **Crown:** ≈ 22 plates, two prongs each ≈ 2.5 × 3 studs, tapering, with an open slot ≈ 1 stud wide between them.
  - Diagrid hinted with **Bar 1 × 4 × 2 `6187`** or grille tiles.
  - Prongs topped with **Tile 1 × 2 with Console** or curved slope **`50950`**.
- **Podium (Lotte World Mall):**
  - ≈ 12 × 10 × 6 plates, White, curved ends with **Brick 4 × 4 Corner Round `48092`/`2577`** and **Slope Curved 6 × 1 `42022`** eaves;
  - a lawn and a figure in front.
- **Part estimate:** shaft ≈ 450, crown ≈ 60, podium ≈ 150; **≈ 650 parts, footprint ≈ 12 × 10.**

**Echo of Shanghai:** Shanghai Tower is a pale twisting supertall. LWT must not be grey-blue glass or it reads as a duplicate. **White + seam + slotted crown** keeps it distinct.

## 3. DDP: brick versions
- **None found.** No DDP model on Rebrickable, The Brothers Brick (Korea tag), Flickr or Korean search; LEGOLAND Korea's Dongdaemun model is the gate (Heunginjimun).
- Maxtud's Micro Seoul contains a low grey/blue piece that *might* be DDP; unconfirmed.
- **Nearest technique** (other Zaha Hadid buildings): LEGO Ideas "Heydar Aliyev Center" builds the flowing roof as **a strip of hinged plates bent around the façade**, fixed on one side only.
  - The search snippet names part 60471 = **Hinge Plate 1 × 2 Locking 9-Position with Dual Finger on Side** and part 44567 (→ `44567a`, the single-finger mate).
  - That works for a ribbon, not for DDP's doubly-curved pillow edges.
- **If it went in:**
  - ≈ 20 × 14 × 9 plates in **Flat Silver / Light Bluish Gray**;
  - edges from **Brick 5 × 5 Corner Round with Curved Top `24599`**, **Slope Curved 8 × 2 × 2 `41766`**, **2 × 8 × 2 Double `11290`**, and **inverted `11301` / `13547`** for the underside flare;
  - **Tile 4 × 4 Corner Round `27507`** on the roof, with a green lawn;
  - ≈ 500 parts, and no proven build to copy (the rule after two failed rounds).
- **Verdict: out.**

## 5. Gwanghwamun Square: what exists
1. **LEGO Architecture 21016 Sungnyemun** (2012, official; Adam Reed Tucker, The Brothers Brick)
   - Seoul's other great gate in micro: a stone base with an arch, and a two-tier tiled roof.
   - **Lesson:** the same gate type as Gwanghwamun; its roof profile and stone base are the vocabulary to scale up.
2. **LEGOLAND Korea Miniland Seoul**
   - Lists **Gwanghwamun Plaza** and **Gyeongbokgung**; the palace took 100 builders three months (newswire 2022).
   - Miniland sculpts statues and figures from small bricks. No photo obtained.
3. **Hyundai Department Store LEGO exhibition** (Dec 2014; Segye Ilbo, fnnews)
   - 290 fan works, including a **brick Admiral Yi statue** and the 63 Building. No image.
4. **"Let's Play Gwanghwamun" (LEGO Korea × Seoul, May 2026)**
   - A brick **King Sejong throne** to sit in, a 12 × 12 m mosaic of landmarks from all 25 districts (LEGO.com news, Nate).
- **Our scale:**
  - **Gwanghwamun gate:** ≈ 14 × 7 studs, ≈ 24 plates to the ridge. A dressed-stone base (Light Bluish Gray / Tan) with **three arches** (arch moulds, e.g. `6005`/`18653` family). Two-tier hip roof in Dark Bluish Gray via `hipCorners`, with dancheong eaves in **Dark Green / Sand Green + Dark Red** plate bands. ≈ 400 parts. Bugaksan as a green rise behind if the outline allows.

  - The square: pale granite tiles with floor-fountain jets (`30374` lightsaber bars, Trans-Clear).
  - **Echo of Kyoto:** a traditional gate with a tiled roof. Korean roofs differ in their steeper concave curve and dancheong, so keep the gate grey-green-red, not Kyoto's palette.

## Borrow / avoid
**Borrow**
- **LWT:**
  - White, continuous convex taper via plate-thickness SNOT setbacks, a **dark seam**, and a **two-prong slotted crown** ≈ 22 % of height (every MOC agrees);
  - a low white curved mall podium.
- **Han:**
  - a wide calm band **across** the island, crossed by **Banpo over Jamsu** (paired piers, low railed lower deck);
  - the **Hangang Bus catamaran** (white, blue gradient);
  - park life on one bank: chimaek mat, delivery rider, cycle path with yellow line, ramyeon kiosk.
- **Gwanghwamun:** the gate as the anchor (21016 vocabulary), and a gold seated Sejong in front facing south.

**Avoid**
- Trans-blue/grey glass on LWT (Shanghai echo; renderer frost). Stepped Art-Deco setbacks.
- A rainbow-coloured fountain by day. Flexible hoses (they bake straight).
- A river front-to-back with towers on both banks (Shanghai's composition).
- DDP and Cheonggyecheon as extra places: no proven build in one case, and a second water body in the other.
- Swan boats (no mould; minor in reality).
