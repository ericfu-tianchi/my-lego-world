# Shikumen lane (石库门弄堂): references and brick versions

2026-10-02. Research only. Every image in this folder was looked at before it was described. Measurements are pixel ratios taken from the photographs (method given per bullet), so they are ±10–15 % because of perspective. Anything I could not verify is marked **unverified**.

**Building type.** There are two body types, and choosing between them is a real decision:
- (a) **Grey brick with red bands, carved pediments, grey tile roof.** This is Xintiandi/树德里 and Siwenli, the image in the brief.
- (b) **All red brick, plain or brick-arched frames, red-brown machine tiles.** This is Bugaoli and Jianyeli (Wikipedia: Bugaoli has a "red brick exterior"; Jianyeli is "all two-storey red brick").
- **Bugaoli is type (b).** Its name on a type (a) grey lane would be a mismatch (see Lettering).

**Proportions** (sources, then my photo measurements):
- **Door.** Typical door is about **1.4 m wide × 2.8 m high**, two leaves in black lacquer (en/zh Wikipedia "Shikumen", which agree).
- **Bay width (客堂).** About **4 m**. This comes from a secondary search snippet; I did not find a primary survey, so treat it as **unverified**. Measured on `real-door-frame-flat-pediment.jpg` and `Shikumen.jpg`:
  - The door opening is about **⅓ of the bay**.
  - The stone frame, including its pilasters, is about **½ of the bay**.
- **Storey height** is about the same as the bay width. Measured on `real-lane-arch-jianyeli.jpg` against the pilaster spacing: ground storey ≈ 1.1 × bay, upper storey ≈ 0.9 × bay. The door opening fills about **70 %** of the ground-storey height.
- **Pediment.** The top of the pediment reaches the **upper-storey window sill**, so the frame plus pediment is a single tall feature that spans the floor line (`Shikumen.jpg`, `real-house-front-pediments-dormers.jpg`).
- **Pediment shapes.** All four appear in the photos:
  - semicircular, with a carved cartouche and scrolls (Xintiandi; carved brick, painted dark red in the restoration);
  - triangular, with a shell or scroll;
  - flat entablature with dentils, plus shell-and-drop reliefs on the pilasters (`real-door-frame-flat-pediment.jpg`);
  - segmental or broken curved (`real-door-rongkangli-curved-pediment.jpg`).
  - Early lanes have a plain stone lintel; later lanes add a red-brick relieving arch (Bugaoli).
- **Red-band rhythm**, counted course by course on the left wall of `Shikumen.jpg` at full resolution:
  - **2 red courses, then about 14 grey courses: a 16-course repeat**, which is about 1.1 m if a course is about 70 mm.
  - Bands line up with the **door springing / impost line** and roughly **knee height**, so about **two to three per storey**.
  - Window heads are **red-brick segmental arches**, and some reveals are edged in red.
  - At Siwenli and in `real-door-frame-flat-pediment.jpg` the bands are **1 course** and further apart.
  - The red is a deep brick red, darker than Bright Red: **Dark Red**, with Reddish Brown where it is weathered.
- **Grey brick.** It is blue-black and varies brick to brick: mostly **Dark Bluish Gray**, with **Black** and **Light Bluish Gray** bricks scattered through. White mortar shows as fine lines.
- **Frame stone colour.**
  - Early (Ningbo/Suzhou stone): warm pinkish-beige granite, **Tan** (`Shikumen.jpg`) or **Dark Tan** (Rongkangli).
  - Later (cement or terrazzo, 汰石子): **Light Bluish Gray** (Siwenli, Bugaoli).
  - Doors: **Black**, with brass knockers.
- **Roof pitch.** Measured on the triangular gable `real-gable-bugaoli-triangular-pitch.jpg` from apex to eave: right slope ≈ 26°, left ≈ 21° (perspective). Call it **≈ 25° ± 5°**.
  - **Roofing.** Grey 小青瓦 pantiles at Xintiandi; red-brown machine tiles at Bugaoli and Jianyeli.
  - **Dormers** are small gabled boxes, about **one per bay or every other bay** (Bugaoli: 3 dormers over about 5–6 bays).
  - **晒台** is a rear flat-roof terrace with a parapet.
  - **Gables:** plain triangle, stepped horse-head, or baroque curved and stepped.
- **Lane width and height.** Early lanes are about **3 m** wide; Bugaoli's main lane is about **2.5 m** (Wikipedia). With eaves at about 7–8 m, **height : width ≈ 2.5–3**, which reads as a slot (`real-lane-siwenli-grey-brick.jpg`).
- **Laundry.**
  - Bamboo poles (晾衣竹竿) rest on **fixed iron or concrete racks spanning the lane at upper-storey height**. Siwenli has roughly one rack per house.
  - Poles also rest on **wall-mounted Y/T brackets** under the upper windows (`real-lane-laundry-poles.jpg`).
  - The bamboo is pale tan-grey, so **Tan / Dark Tan**, not black.
- **Scale check.** At minifig scale (1 stud ≈ 0.36 m, 1 brick ≈ 0.44 m):
  - A true-scale bay is about 11 studs, and a storey about 8–9 bricks.
  - The brief's 5–6-stud bay and 24–30-plate total is **≈ 2× horizontal and ≈ 2.5× vertical compression**.
  - **A real minifig door (1×4×6 frame) is 18 plates on its own**, which is 60–75 % of a 24–30-plate building, before the stone lintel, pediment, band, upper storey and roof. See Borrow / avoid.

**Plane tree (法国梧桐).**
- **Trunk:** a clear bole about one storey tall, then a **V of 2–3 pollarded limbs**.
- **Bark:** cream, white, olive-grey and grey-green **patches** (`real-gable-bugaoli-triangular-pitch.jpg` and `real-lane-arch-jianyeli.jpg` show it best).
- **Height:** the crown tops out around the eaves of a 3-storey building.
- **Late-autumn crown** (`real-plane-trees-autumn-jingan.jpg`) is **ochre / olive / yellow-brown, not saturated yellow**. Suggested LEGO colours: Olive Green + Dark Tan + Medium Nougat, with Bright Light Orange accents. china.md's Yellow / Bright Light Orange / Dark Orange is louder than the real tree.

**Lettering on lane arches** (what the photos show):
- **步高里** (`real-lane-arch-bugaoli-lettering-crop.jpg`):
  - Arch body: cream / ochre stucco with a speckled finish.
  - Name: three **dark (black-grey) regular-script (楷) characters** in a **recessed, moulded rectangular panel** over the arch. They are raised or inset; I can't tell which at this resolution.
  - Above the panel: **CITÉ BOURGOGNE** in dark serif capitals, and **1930** in a roundel at the top.
  - The characters read left to right.
- **建業里總衖** (`real-lane-arch-jianyeli.jpg`): five **raised, verdigris-green characters** in heavy 楷 on the grey stucco frieze over the gate, reading **right to left**.
- **廣興里** (`real-lane-plaque-guangxingli-tangtuo.jpg`):
  - Three large **raised black 颜体-style 楷 characters** on a whitewashed moulded panel, reading **right to left**.
  - A small 北二 above, and the signature 武進唐駝 in small characters at the side.
- zh-Wikipedia: lane names "一般都会与所建的年份一起刻在里弄弄口的过街楼上", meaning the name and the year are carved on the lane-mouth arch.
- **Pattern:** 3 characters (sometimes 4–5 with 總衖) plus a year, in dark or green relief, in a moulded panel, in calligraphic 楷. Never painted signage, never red-on-gold.

## 1. 上海石库门·屋里厢 (2020): G.S / GegoVs (钱永杰, Qian Yj)
- **This is the "乐乐镇 2020" build.** It was G.S's entry to the 乐乐镇 LUG 2020 "大基建 · 消失的民居" contest. 乐乐镇 is the RLUG, not the builder.
- URLs:
  - https://www.163.com/dy/article/G1MSSL3K0526S7MV.html
  - https://www.heroeslug.com/2021/01/31/gsmoc_skm/
  - Flickr set "Shanghai Skikumen Wulixiang", https://www.flickr.com/photos/138602454@N07/ (2021-01-30)
  - Compilation: https://www.sohu.com/a/747947206_120985308
  - lelezhen.com (the original thread) returned 522.
- Size 39 × 77 × 42 cm. The house is a **double-bay** (双开间) with one wall left open; the interior follows the Shikumen Open House Museum.
- Files: `moc-gs-2020-wulixiang-front-pediment.jpg`, `moc-gs-2020-wulixiang-door-frame.jpg`, `moc-gs-2020-wulixiang-lane-elevation-bands.jpg`, `moc-gs-2020-wulixiang-rear-roof.jpg`, `moc-gs-2020-wulixiang-shaitai-laundry.jpg`.
- **Wall.** Light and Dark Bluish Gray **masonry-profile bricks and offset 1×2 / 1×4 tiles**, with white 1×1 / 1×2 tiles scattered in as lighter bricks. It reads as brick at a glance, but **too light and too blotchy** next to the real blue-black wall.
- **Bands.** **One plate of Dark Red / Red with a Reddish Brown plate under it**, about every 4–5 bricks: two bands per storey plus a cornice band. The rhythm reads right; at our scale the double plate band would be too thick.
- **Door frame.**
  - Light Bluish Gray and White **fluted pilasters** (grille-tile faces).
  - **Volute capitals** made of small round parts with holes, two pairs per pilaster.
  - A **triangular pediment** of slopes with a relief cartouche built from small clips and bars.
  - Black double door leaves with pearl-gold knockers.
  - This is the best read of a shikumen frame I found, but the frame is cool grey, not warm stone.
- **Gables.** Stepped / baroque gables in grey brick with Light Bluish Gray coping. Very legible.
- **Windows.** White frames with curved heads and keystones.
- **Roof.** **Staggered rows of 1×2 / 2×2 plates in Orange / Bright Light Orange laid studs-up and stepped**: a pantile texture at about one plate per stud. That is ≈ 22°, close to the real ≈ 25°. Dormers are white-sided gabled boxes.
- **晒台 laundry.**
  - Two **black bars** carried by clips on **Reddish Brown bar posts** at the corners of the terrace parapet.
  - Clothes are coloured **flags / cloth / tiles hanging from clips** on the bars, plus a hanging fish.
  - Reads immediately.
- **Lesson:** the signature is the **door frame + pediment as one tall stone object**, bands of Dark Red plate, a stepped gable and laundry on bars over a parapet. Copy all four. Avoid the light-grey wall and the orange roof for a type (a) lane.

## 2. Beijing Courtyard House (2023) and Xiaohe Street (2022): G.S
- URL: https://www.flickr.com/photos/138602454@N07/ (sets "Beijing Courtyard House", "Xiaohe Street Historyic Block").
- Files: `moc-gs-2023-beijing-courtyard-autumn-tree-grey-wall.jpg`, `moc-gs-2022-xiaohe-street-grey-tile-roof.jpg`.
- **Grey brick wall.**
  - Light Bluish Gray wall with **1×2 / 1×3 / 1×4 tiles mounted studs-out at half-plate offsets**, so the courses look staggered. A Dark Bluish Gray plinth.
  - Window grilles are green ornamental fence parts.
  - Calmer and more convincing than the masonry-brick mix in §1.
- **Grey tile roof.** Rows of Black and Dark Bluish Gray **ridged tiles / grille-type 1×2 parts in alternating colour rows**. The eave is a line of small round tiles or plates as the tile-end discs (瓦当).
- **Autumn tree.**
  - A Reddish Brown trunk of round bricks and bar / hose sections.
  - The crown is **clustered round leaf / flower parts in Bright Light Orange and Orange**.
  - Single **round 1×1 plates in the same colours scattered on the pavement** as fallen leaves.
- **Lesson:**
  - **Studs-out offset tiles** for a fine brick bond.
  - **Two-colour rows** for a grey pantile roof.
  - Round 1×1 plates as fallen leaves on the lane: a cheap, legible autumn signal.

## 4. "laundry" (Italian house, 2026): Wolfgang Binder
- URL: https://www.flickr.com/photos/wollebinder/55173314110/
- File: `moc-wolfgangbinder-2026-laundry-pantile-roof.jpg`.
- **Pantile roof.**
  - **Rows of 2×1 curved slopes in Orange / Medium Nougat / Bright Light Orange, laid down the slope with gaps.**
  - The **Reddish Brown plate studs show between the rows** as the pan channels.
  - The best pantile I found. In Dark Bluish Gray curved slopes on Black plates it becomes 小青瓦.
- **Wall laundry.**
  - **A Light Bluish Gray bar held at both ends by bar-holder clips** standing off the wall.
  - Laundry is a **printed 2×2 tile with clip** and a **cloth cape** draped over the bar.
- **Wall.** Mixed brick colours in two or three close shades.
- **Lesson:** curved-slope rows give a true pantile at minifig scale; a bar between two clips is the whole laundry line. **Our prints rule** (≤ 4 flat colours) allows a plain-coloured cloth or tile; one patterned print is optional.

## Borrow / avoid
**Borrow**
- **Door frame + pediment as one tall stone element**, from the step up to the upper sill, in Tan (granite type) or Light Bluish Gray (cement type):
  - flat pilasters with fluting;
  - small volute capitals;
  - one pediment shape per house, alternating along the terrace (semicircle / triangle / flat with dentils);
  - Black two-leaf door (§1, real photos).
- **Wall:** Dark Bluish Gray body with scattered Black and Light Bluish Gray bricks.
  - **Fine bond** from studs-out offset 1×2 / 1×4 tiles (§2), or masonry-profile bricks (§1).
  - **One Dark Red plate band at the door impost line and one at the sill line per storey**, on whole plate layers measured from `base`. This keeps real alignment rather than real course count.
  - Dark Red segmental heads over windows.
- **Roof:**
  - **Pantiles** from rows of 2×1 curved slopes in Dark Bluish Gray on Black plates, with the studs showing between rows (§4);
  - a **row of 1×1 round bricks end-on** as the eave discs (§3);
  - pitch ≈ one plate per stud (≈ 22°, real ≈ 25°);
  - one small gabled dormer per bay;
  - one stepped gable at the lane-mouth end.
- **Laundry:** a **Tan / Dark Tan bar (bamboo) between two clips**, spanning the lane at upper-storey height on a fixed rack, or wall brackets under a window. Two or three plain-colour cloth / flag / clip-tile pieces (§1, §4).
- **Lane-mouth arch:**
  - a moulded panel over a round arch;
  - **three dark 楷 characters in relief**, with a small year roundel above;
  - right-to-left reading is period-correct.
- **Plane tree:**
  - **Mottled trunk of Tan / White / Light Bluish Gray / Olive Green pieces** (Brick Worm), forking into 2–3 limbs above one storey;
  - **mixed ochre crown** (Olive Green, Dark Tan, Medium Nougat, Bright Light Orange);
  - a few round 1×1 plates as fallen leaves.

**Avoid**
- Light Bluish Gray as the wall body: it reads as concrete (§1).
- Orange or red machine tiles on a grey type (a) lane.
- A saturated yellow / orange crown.
- Black bars as bamboo.
- Red-on-gold or typeset lettering.
- Squeezing a 1×4×6 minifig door into a 24–30-plate house.

**Decisions for Eric**
1. **Body type.** Grey + red bands (Xintiandi / Siwenli, the brief's image), or red brick (Bugaoli / Jianyeli).
   - **If grey, the arch should not say 步高里**: Bugaoli is a red-brick lane with a cream arch.
   - Grey-type lanes I could photograph: 树德里 (First Congress), 斯文里. Neither of their arch inscriptions is verified.
   - **If 步高里 stays**, match it: red brick, cream arch, red-brown tiles.
2. **Height.** With a real minifig door, the ground storey alone needs about 22–24 plates (door 18 + lintel / pediment + band), so the house becomes **≈ 36–42 plates**, not 24–30. That is still under the plane tree if the tree grows to about 45. The alternative is to drop the second storey to a half-storey under the roof.
