## 1. Which group is on top (autumn 2026)
**BTS: the comeback is complete and the tour is running.**
- **Album:** *ARIRANG* (5th studio album, released 20 March 2026). Debuted **No. 1 on the Billboard 200** with **641,000 units** (532,000 pure sales, the biggest group sales week since 2013) and held No. 1 for three weeks. It also topped the Hot 100 at the same time. Sources: [weareresonate](https://www.weareresonate.com/2026/03/bts-returns-to-no-1-on-the-billboard-200-with-record-sales-for-arirang), [SBS Star](https://sbsstar.net/article/N1008515684/bts-rules-billboard-200-for-3-straight-weeks-with-arirang-world-tour-off-to-a-hot-start), [Bandwagon](https://www.bandwagon.asia/articles/bts-score-simultaneous-no-1-debut-on-billboard-hot-100-and-200-with-arirang). fnnews reports 3.98 M copies sold on day one in Korea.
- **Comeback concert:** **21 March 2026, Gwanghwamun Square**, free, about one hour, ≈ 104,000 on site, live on Netflix in 190 countries ([AP via news4jax](https://www.news4jax.com/entertainment/2026/03/19/bts-will-stage-a-long-awaited-comeback-concert-at-a-seoul-landmark/), [fnnews](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202603220744455518), [Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/en/article/202603220730007)). See §3.
- **Arirang World Tour:** 9 April 2026 (Goyang Stadium, 3 nights, 127,218 attendance) to 16 March 2027, 88 shows in 34 cities. Busan Asiad followed on 12–13 June. From September to December 2026 the tour is in Latin America and Southeast Asia ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arirang_World_Tour)). In August 2026 it grossed **$171.7 M** in one month, the second-highest month in Boxscore history ([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/en/article/202608290811007)). It is the **first pop-group tour past $500 M**, as of 30 Sep 2026 ([netflixjunkie / Billboard](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/music-news-bts-achieves-monumental-feat-as-arirang-tour-reaches-new-milestone/)).
- **Others:** BLACKPINK (*DEADLINE* sold a record 1.46 M on its first day for a girl group), Stray Kids (eight No. 1 albums in a row on the Billboard 200 [aggregator, not primary]), SEVENTEEN, TWICE. A fan-site roundup cites the Billboard K-Pop 100 / Korea brand-reputation index for February 2026: BTS #1, BLACKPINK #2, NewJeans #3, Stray Kids #4 ([X post](https://x.com/TXT_DailyNews/status/2025023971968893082); secondary, treat as indicative).
- **Recommendation: BTS.** It leads on every 2026 measure found, and it is the only act with a 2026 concert *at a landmark that is already on our island*. Seven members is also a count people recognise.

## 2. Official LEGO precedent and likeness
### 2.1 LEGO Ideas 21339 BTS Dynamite (2023) · `lego-21339-1.jpg`, `ne-21339-*.jpg`
- **749 pieces, $99.99.** It builds the *Dynamite* music-video street: a **DISCO** façade, a record shop ("Sweet Music"), a donut shop with a giant purple donut, an ice-cream truck and a basketball hoop, plus a separate **black dance stage** ([Brickset](https://brickset.com/article/92025/review-21339-bts-dynamite), [New Elementary](https://www.newelementary.com/2023/02/lego-ideas-review-21339-bts-dynamite.html)).
- **The stage** (`ne-21339-stage-side.jpg`): a black box about 12 × 8 studs. The seven figures stand on axle-end studs that are geared together underneath, so turning a side gear makes them "dance" in sync. There is **one printed BTS-logo tile** on the front, and the figures hold **microphones**. There are no screens or truss. Four sticker sheets are used for the shop signs.

### 2.2 Likeness options (personal, non-commercial site)
| Option | What | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Recognisable members** | 21339-style hair mould and colour per member, outfit colours, no names | Reads as BTS to any fan. But it is a deliberate likeness of seven named real people, which is what LEGO needed a licence for, and it breaks our own LA precedent (§1.2: "prints of real people are out"). The risk on a private hobby site is low [M, not legal advice], but the rule is ours, and "it's only a hobby" is the exception Eric refused for Kobe. |
| **(b) Generic idol group implying BTS** ✅ | **Seven** figures in **matching stage outfits** (a K-pop group is dressed as a unit; 2026 key colour **red**, Joseon military-inspired costumes per fnnews), generic LEGO smile faces, hair moulds chosen for variety but **not** matched member by member. Context does the rest: the **real comeback stage** on the **real square** in front of the **real gate**, **purple light sticks**, one fan with a **purple heart** sign | Truthful and in line with our rules, and it still reads "BTS at Gwanghwamun" to anyone who followed March 2026. Purple is fan culture (*borahae* 보라해, "I purple you", is a phrase coined by V in 2016 [M]), not a trademark. |
| (c) Names or logos printed | "BTS", the logo, member names, a printed ARMY Bomb logo | **No.** The BTS name and logo are trademarks of BIGHIT MUSIC / HYBE [M]. Printing them is trademark use, and rule 1 L2 allows only "generic words" anyway. |

**Recommendation: (b).** Two details are allowed because they carry no name: (1) the light sticks are **white globes on black handles**, the ARMY Bomb *shape* (photographed, `real-armybomb.jpg`), lit purple; (2) the red **"●●●" Arirang mark** on the event signage is an album graphic, so **leave it out** to stay clean. "WELCOME BTS ♥ ARMY" banners (`real-welcome-army-banner.jpg`) are also out.

## 3. Where K-pop lives in real Seoul, and the placement
| Site | Fact | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Gwanghwamun Square** ✅ | **21 Mar 2026: BTS THE COMEBACK LIVE: ARIRANG**. A **frame-shaped open-cube stage** by Florian Wieder and Guy Carrington, directed by Hamish Hamilton, built so that "Gwanghwamun is held inside the frame" (j-hope; [Herald](https://www.heraldk.com/article/2026032023280136604)). There was a three-layer LED cube based on the flag's trigrams, media art projected on the gate's walls, and ARMY Bombs synced to the show ([fnnews](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202603220744455518), [fanplus](https://fanplus.co.kr/en-US/community/bts/127491616)). The city added ten media-façade screens around the square ([Seoul Mediahub](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/2017532)) | **Best.** The gate and Sejong are already on our island |
| Jamsil Olympic Stadium | Seoul's stadium-concert venue, by Lotte World Tower [M]. 2026 Korea dates were at Goyang and Busan, not Jamsil (Jamsil main stadium is under renovation [M]) | Would put a stadium on the island: too big, and not 2026-true |
| Hangang parks | Picnics and busking | Already the chimaek life layer |
| Hongdae | Street busking (`../seoul-hanok/real-hongdae-busking.jpg`) | Not on the island; reads only "a singer" |
| HYBE HQ, Yongsan | Office tower, fan pilgrimage | An office building, not readable |

**Real layout** (`real-aerial-stage-gate.jpg`, `real-aerial-frame-gate-bugaksan-2.jpg`, `real-frame-gate-through.jpg`):
- North to south: **Bugaksan → Gwanghwamun gate → Yulgok-ro (the road in front of the gate) → the stage → seating down the square past Sejong to the Admiral Yi statue** (`real-seats-yi-sejongcenter.jpg`).
- The **frame faces south** with the gate behind it. It stands at the square's north end, slightly west of the gate's axis.
- **The frame:**
  - a near-square **LED box** about as tall as the gate [M, from photos], with a square opening ≈ 0.64 of its width and ≈ 0.73 of its height. The top band is deeper than the sides (≈ 27 % of height vs ≈ 18 %);
  - skin: a **diagonal checker / chevron of Red and grey** LED tiles, edged in black;
  - inside: a **lighting truss** with a row of hanging moving-head fixtures, and **vertical LED strips** in purple, teal and blue down the inner sides (`real-frame-side.jpg`, `real-frame-lighting-rig.jpg`).
- **On each side:** a **black truss tower carrying a hung line-array speaker column**, taller than the frame.
- **In front:** a low **black stage deck** with a short **thrust runway** and black crowd barriers. Behind them the seating is in rows.
- **Placement on our island:** the vignette sits **between the Gwanghwamun gate and Sejong**, on the square's paving. The frame stands on the gate's axis so the camera sees the gate's arches through the opening. Being offset like the real one is allowed if it frees the view.
- The royal-guard figure (`../seoul-hanok/NOTES.md` §6) should then be cut: the gate's "life" is now the concert.

## 4. Brick versions (how others build stages)
1. **21339 BTS Dynamite (2023, official)**, `lego-21339-1.jpg`, `ne-21339-stage-side.jpg`
   - A black box stage, seven figures in a row with microphones, and a gear-linked "dance".
   - **Borrow:** seven figures shoulder to shoulder on a black deck read as a boy band even without a screen; handheld mics.
2. **41105 Pop Star Show Stage (Friends, 2015, official)**, `lego-41105-1.jpg`
   - **Black lattice truss pillars** (girder supports) carrying a **lighting bar of 1 × 1 round trans studs** and two **2 × 2 speakers with round grilles**.
   - A **central printed screen tile** framed by coloured round lights, plus wing screens.
   - **Borrow:** the black girder towers, the row of trans round 1 × 1 lights under the top beam, a speaker cabinet = brick + round tile/dish.
3. **41106 Pop Star Tour Bus (Friends, 2015, official)**, `lego-41106-1.jpg`
   - Two free-standing **speaker towers**: girder support, stacked speaker boxes, and a light on top.
   - **Borrow:** a speaker tower as one girder plus stacked 1 × 2 grille bricks, which is the line-array look.
4. **42616 Heartlake City Music Talent Show (Friends, 2024, official)**, `lego-42616-1.png`
   - **Red girder towers** holding giant speakers. **Clip-light spotlights** on bars are aimed at the stage, and **trans-pink lightsaber-blade beams** fan out from the stage edge.
   - **Borrow:** clip lights on the frame's inner beam. Beams of light-saber bars are tempting, but they would read as clutter at island scale: **avoid**.
5. **41254 Trolls Volcano Rock City Concert (2020, official)**, `lego-41254-1.jpg`
   - A **black lattice truss** portal, and an arched purple truss of girder-arch moulds, with speakers built as **bricks with round tiles** and a **studs-out backdrop of tiles making a picture** (the big blue face).
   - **Borrow:** **the screen image is a studs-out tile mosaic**, not a printed panel. This is our frame technique.
6. **70828 Pop-Up Party Bus (LEGO Movie 2, 2019)** and **41103 Pop Star Recording Studio (2015)**, `lego-70828-1.jpg`, `lego-41103-1.jpg`
   - Minor: microphones on stands, headphones, round-tile speakers.
- **MOC search:** "LEGO concert stage MOC", "LEGO BTS MOC" and "레고 BTS 창작 무대" turned up LEGO Ideas concert stages (truss, backline, barrier, 360° stage; no images fetched) and a Queen Live-Aid MOC. **No MOC of the 2026 Gwanghwamun frame exists yet.** The frame is a simple shape, so the real photos are the reference.

**Technique summary:**
- **Frame skin:** a **studs-out mosaic** of 1 × 1 tiles on **bricks with studs on the side** (`30414` 1 × 4, `87087` 1 × 1, `26604` 1 × 1 two sides) or **brackets** (`99780` / `99781`, `99207`). Colours: **Red / Dark Red / Dark Bluish Gray / Light Bluish Gray** in a diagonal checker, **Black** edges. *No print and no photo*: the real skin is already a geometric LED pattern, which is the honest version of "a screen".
- **Inner LED strips:** vertical runs of `3070b` tiles in **Medium Lavender / Dark Purple / Dark Turquoise / Dark Azure**.
- **Lighting rig:** **`4081b` Plate 1 × 1 with Clip Light** (Black) hung under the inner top beam, a row of 6–8 fixtures; or `6141` round plates in trans colours under a black 1 × N beam.
- **Speaker towers:** **`30646` Support 2 × 2 × 8 Girder** or **`64448` Support 1 × 6 × 5 Girder Rectangular** / **`64449` 1 × 6 × 10 Triangular** (Black), carrying a **line array** = a column of **`2877` Brick 1 × 2 with Grille** (Black), slightly taller than the frame.
- **Stage deck:** Black plates and tiles 2–3 plates high, with a 2-wide thrust. Stairs go at the back.
- **Barrier:** **`3633` Fence Lattice 1 × 4 × 1** in Black or Light Bluish Gray.

## 5. Proposed vignette (minifig scale)
**"Comeback night at Gwanghwamun"**: the frame on the square, the gate seen through it, seven idols mid-move on the deck, two fans at the barrier holding up purple light sticks.

```
 N   ─── Gwanghwamun gate (existing element, ≈14 × 7) ─── Yulgok-ro paving ───
      [spk]  ┌────────── frame 12 wide × 2 deep ──────────┐  [spk]
      tower  │ mosaic │      opening 8 × ~19 plates      │ mosaic │  tower
      2×2    │        │  clip lights under the top beam  │        │  2×2
             └────────┴──────────────────────────────────┴────────┘
             ████████ black deck 12 × 4, 3 plates high, 7 idols ████████
                             ██ thrust 2 × 3 ██
             ─ ─ ─ barrier (fence 1 × 4 × 1 ×3) ─ ─ ─   2 fans + light sticks
 S                       … square paving → Sejong statue
```
- **Footprint:** ≈ **14 × 12 studs** (frame plus towers 14 wide; deck, thrust, barrier and fans 12 deep). It is cut from the Gwanghwamun Square area, not added to it.
- **Heights (fix in the set's height table):**
  - frame top ≈ **26 plates** above the square, about the gate's 24-plate ridge, as in the photos;
  - speaker towers ≈ 30 plates;
  - **both stay far below Lotte World Tower (the hero) and N Seoul Tower.** If the frame competes with the gate in the overall shot, drop it to ≈ 22 plates. Decide in a render.
  - The opening ≈ 8 studs × ≈ 19 plates frames the gate's three arches from the low "hero" camera. From the default elevated camera the gate's roof shows over and through it. **Check in the first render.**
- **The screen, without a photo:** the frame's own **red/grey diagonal LED mosaic** (§4) on the south face. The back face is plain black. **No print needed.** If one print is wanted, put it on a 2 × 2 tile at the centre of the top band: a **purple heart** (≤ 2 colours) on black.
- **Idols:** 7 (the count is the identifier).
  - Matching stage outfits: a **Black** torso with **Dark Red** Joseon-military-style front panels (an L2 torso print, one design for all seven: ≤ 4 colours, gold buttons);
  - **Black** legs and Light Nougat heads with plain smile faces;
  - varied hair, in black / dark brown / one Dark Tan / one coloured.
  - Two hold `90370` mics. Poses vary with the `minifig2` arm poses: arms up, one pointing out.
  - ⚠ The real outfit colours were not confirmed in a performance photo (Commons has only pre-show shots). Only "red key colour" and "Joseon military garments" are sourced (fnnews).
- **Fans:** 2 at the barrier with light sticks raised, back three-quarter to camera (watching the stage is the action, rule 6). One holds a 1 × 2 tile sign with a **purple heart**, not a name: a real fan sign shows "정국", a member's name (`real-fan-name-sign.jpg`), which is out.
- **Figure count:** 7 + 2 = **9**, the Shanghai precedent. With the river chimaek pair and a hanok figure the island holds ≈ 12. **Eric's call:** 9 on the stage, or 4 idols + 1 fan (reads "a K-pop group", loses "BTS").
- **Part estimate:**

| Item | Parts |
|---|---|
| Frame (studs-out mosaic ≈ 85 tiles, ≈ 30 side-stud bricks / brackets, ≈ 70 black structure, inner LED strips ≈ 20) | ≈ 210 |
| Lighting rig (8 clip lights + beam) | ≈ 20 |
| Speaker towers ×2 (girder + 1 × 2 grille column + cap) | ≈ 40 |
| Deck + thrust + stairs | ≈ 70 |
| Barrier + paving tiles under the vignette | ≈ 40 |
| 7 idols + 2 fans (≈ 6–7 parts each with mics, sticks, sign) | ≈ 60 |
| **Total** | **≈ 440** (range 420–520) |

- **Structure (rule 2, "nothing just lies on anything"):**
  - The frame is an exploded assembly. The mosaic hangs on real side studs.
  - The inner top beam spans the opening as **two staggered plate layers**, gripped at both ends.
  - Clip lights hang from studs under that beam.
  - The towers stand on studs.
