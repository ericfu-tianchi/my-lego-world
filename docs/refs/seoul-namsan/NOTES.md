**Published figures** (en.wikipedia "N Seoul Tower", structurae / skyscraperpage via search): tower **236.7 m** (base to tip, incl. antenna); tip at **479.7 m a.s.l.** → **tower foot at ≈ 243 m a.s.l.** (the hilltop); **antenna 101 m**; observation level **≈ 136 m**; "max shaft diameter 30 m, min 10 m" (the 30 m figure fits the base/pod better than the shaft — [verify]). Built 1969–75, opened to the public 1980. Love locks since 2006. Cable car since 1962. Palgakjeong built 1959, rebuilt 1968. Downtown at its foot lies at ≈ 20–40 m a.s.l. [M], so the hill's relief above the city (~200 m) is about the same as the tower's height.

- **Plan: round everywhere.** Shaft, lower cylinder, both pod drums, collar: all circular (every photo from every side gives the same silhouette; `locks-ntower01.jpg` from below shows circular rings and a circular underside). Rule "round things are round" applies to the whole tower.
- **Pod structure** (`real-pod-closeup.jpg`, `measure-tower-north-crop.png`): a **shallow dark bowl** underneath (≈ 25 % of pod height) curving into the shaft → **lower drum**, widest, ≈ 4–5 rows of blue-grey glass between white/light-grey floor bands (slightly barrel-bulged) → **upper drum**, set back to ≈ 0.8 Ø, 2 window rows → flat roof disc → narrow **white collar** carrying small dish antennas → mast.
- **Mast**: a steel lattice in **aviation red and white bands**, red at the bottom and at the top, ≈ 7 bands, stepping narrower twice (at small flange rings). The lowest red section is the widest and carries panel antennas.
- **Second mast**: a separate red-white broadcast lattice mast stands ≈ 60 m east of the tower (`real-tower-2026.jpg`, `real-sunset.jpg`). Every brick version omits it. **Recommend: omit** (it dilutes the silhouette).
- **Base**: the tower foot is a **wider white cylinder** with the two radial lattice rings at its top, wrapped on one side by the **base building** (two storeys: white band, full-height glass band, "SEOUL TOWER" lettering — today "N SEOUL TOWER"), with roof terraces where the locks hang (`real-base-plaza.jpg`). In front: a paved plaza with trees, lamp posts, the time-capsule plaza and Palgakjeong a few metres along the ridge (`real-sunset.jpg`: pavilion on the skyline left of the tower).
- **Hill**: Namsan is a **lone, broad, asymmetric dome** above the city (`real-namsan-from-inwangsan.jpg`, `real-hill-from-north.jpg`): rocky granite outcrops on the north face, slopes ≈ 25–30°, **dense mixed forest** — **Korean red pine (sonamu)** on the ridges (the pine of "남산 위에 저 소나무" in the national anthem: the hill's signature tree) among oaks and maples; spring azalea/forsythia bands, autumn reds and oranges. Paved roads/paths zigzag up; the summit is a flat paved plateau.
- **City wall**: the Joseon-era **Seoul City Wall** runs up the ridge to the summit (`real-citywall-ridge.jpg`): a light-grey **granite block wall** stepping up the slope, topped by a **segmented parapet (yeojang)** — merlons ≈ 1.5 m wide with narrow gaps, each with a **pent coping cap** and one or two **square gun holes** (`real-citywall-parapet.jpg`).
- **Cable car**: from Myeong-dong side up the north slope to an upper station just below the plaza; two grey boxy cabins, one tall **lattice pylon** mid-slope (`real-cablecar-*.jpg`).
- **Palgakjeong**: an octagonal open pavilion: 8 **red columns**, painted **green/turquoise dancheong** brackets and eaves, **grey tiled roof** with upturned corners, a finial; on a stepped stone base.
- **Love locks**: thousands of small padlocks (red, pink, yellow, blue, brass) on the **terrace railings, stair handrails and several cone-shaped "lock trees"** ≈ 2–3 m tall.

## 1. LEGOLAND Korea Resort Miniland, Seoul section (Chuncheon, opened 2022), Merlin model shop — Miniland scale (~1:20 by the park's figure)
URL: https://www.flickr.com/photos/shnake1973/54166013864/ · https://www.flickr.com/photos/shnake1973/54166156365/ (Sept 2023) · YouTube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sGzvjFZW6hU · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RrOytQarauA · files `legoland-korea-miniland-seoul.jpg`, `-seoul-2.jpg`, `-tower-crop.png`, `-namsan-yt.jpg`, `-tower-yt.jpg`
- **The only large brick version with the hill.** The tower stands on a real-turf **Namsan** behind Sungnyemun, with a **grey-green sculpted rock face** at the bottom, **Seoul City Wall** in white/pale grey with square crenels running along the ridge on both sides of the tower, the **base building** (white, dark-blue/teal glass band) at the summit, small bluish-grey conifers and benches on the slope, tall real conifers planted around.
- **Proportions** (measured on the crop): pod Ø ≈ 0.12 H, shaft Ø ≈ 0.054 H, mast ≈ 0.38 H, pod + collar ≈ 0.16 H, base building ≈ 0.12 H. **Near-true proportions** — Miniland does not exaggerate the pod.
- **Shaft:** white, smooth, **two flat grey rings near the foot** (the lattice rings), a few small antenna brackets.
- **Pod:** grey drum with dark window rows, a narrower top tier, white collar.
- **Mast:** long, ≈ 3 red + white bands, thin tip.
- Reads well at a distance: the slender white shaft, the banded pod, the long red-white mast, the hill + wall. Weak: the pod is plain at close range; at Miniland size the lattice rings are just discs.
- **Lesson:** this is the composition to copy: **tower on the summit plateau, wall along the ridge, rock face + forest on the slope, base building at the foot.** And keep the shaft slender: true-ish proportions read as N Seoul Tower; fat micro proportions read as "any TV tower".

## Borrow / avoid (our scale: minifig-scale island ≈ 60–70 studs wide; tower is the hero)

**Pick: LEGOLAND Korea Miniland (§1) for the composition and the proportions; the micro builds (§3, §4, §6, §7 sounderTV) for the part techniques of the pod and mast.**

**Proposed tower (Scheme A, recommended): hill plateau 14 plates + tower 82 plates = top at 96 plates above the island ground**, pod Ø 6 studs, shaft Ø 2 studs (pod/shaft 3.0 vs real 2.8 ✓). Pod Ø 6 = 0.18 H (real 0.127: **1.45 × exaggeration**, the same as Shanghai's Pearl spheres); shaft 0.061 H (1.4 ×) — every member exaggerated by the same factor, so the tower keeps its slender shape. Mast shortened to 0.34 H (real 0.43; Miniland 0.38).

- Tower part estimate: base building ≈ 120, tower ≈ 70 → **≈ 190**. Footprint: tower Ø 6 at the pod; foot + base building ≈ 10 × 8.
- Height rule: 96 plates = 38 studs → island ≥ 64 studs wide for "hero ≤ 0.6 × width".

**Scheme B (fits the 60–80 brief): hill 12 + tower 66 = 78 plates.** Same Ø 6 pod and Ø 2 shaft, shaft and mast shortened (pod bottom at 0.36 H, mast 0.27 H). Pod becomes 0.23 H (1.8 ×): a stubbier, micro-build look (§4/§5) — the "any TV tower" risk Miniland avoids. Scheme C (Ø 4 pod on a Ø 2 shaft at 66 plates) keeps the height but the shaft is half the pod: a lollipop. **Recommend A; B only if the island cannot reach 64 studs.**

**Hill (Namsan), ≈ 28 × 22 studs, summit plateau 12 × 10 at +14 plates** (24 × 20 leaves 6 studs of run for 5.6 studs of rise, ≈ 43°: too steep; 28 × 22 gives ≈ 35°):
- **Asymmetric dome** (real): steeper rocky face toward the camera/north (granite outcrops in Light Bluish Gray / Dark Bluish Gray rock bricks and 45°/33° slopes), gentler wooded flank behind.
- **Terraced**, each terrace one brick with the step-backs covered by slopes and curved slopes (Brian Yu §2) — no stud-stepped "pyramid": cover in Green / Olive Green with Dark Green under the pines; one **zigzag path** in Tan/LBG tiles from the cable-car station to the plateau.
- **Trees: 5–7 Korean red pines** (`tree(kind='sonamu')`: new kind, Reddish Brown trunk, Dark Green flat clouds — the anthem pine) on the ridges + 3–4 broadleaf (oak/maple, season colour) + small conifers (`2435`) as Miniland does. Trees stay below the pod (rule 9 tiers).
- **Seoul City Wall**: ≈ 20 studs long, 1 stud thick, climbing one flank to the plateau in plate steps; 2 bricks of Light Bluish Gray embossed bricks + a **segmented parapet** (1×2 merlons with 1-stud gaps, Dark Bluish Gray pent caps, a square gun hole per merlon). Parts ≈ 150.
- **Palgakjeong** on the plateau edge beside the tower: 6×6 octagon, Dark Red columns, Dark Turquoise eaves band, Dark Bluish Gray octagonal roof, cone finial, stepped stone base. Parts ≈ 60.
- **Cable car**: lower station at the island edge, one lattice pylon, braided string ×2, one cabin (≈ 3 × 2 × 5 plates, LBG with dark windows) on the way up. Parts ≈ 60.
- **Hill earth**: solid packed bricks two layers under the surface skin (rule 10) — estimate **≈ 600–700** parts incl. surface slopes; trees ≈ 250.
- **Element total ≈ 1,300–1,400 parts** (tower 190, plateau + locks 60, Palgakjeong 60, wall 150, cable car 60, trees 250, hill 650).

**Love locks: legible?** A real padlock is ≈ 5 cm; at minifig scale that is ≈ 0.15 stud — no single mould can be one. What *can* read is the **mass**: a run of spindled fence densely hung with 1×1 clip tiles in five colours (Red, Bright Pink, Yellow, Medium Azure, Lime) along the terrace edge, plus **one lock tree** (≈ 2×2×5: a core of studs-on-four-sides bricks covered with 1×1 round plates and heart tiles). Verdict: **legible only as "a colourful railing" from the hero camera, and as locks only in close-up when paired with the minifig couple hanging one** (rule 6: the action carries the meaning). Recommend: one 8-stud run + one lock tree + the couple; cut the lock tree if it reads as a flowering shrub in the render.

**Borrow**
- Miniland's composition: tower on the plateau, wall along the ridge, rock face, forest, base building at the foot.
- Near-true slenderness; all tower members exaggerated by one factor (≈ 1.45 ×).
- Pod as **stacked round plates in alternating glass/floor colours** (§3, §4, §6, §7), with a **narrower upper drum** (§6) and a **dark bowl** underneath.
- **Mast = red/white 1×1 round stack + bar** (§3, §4).
- **Two lattice rings at the foot** = webbed dishes (sounderTV §7, Miniland's discs).
- Cable car on a string between posts (§2).

**Avoid**
- Fat shafts (Simplick) and flat-disc pods (Bertie's).
- Trans glass bands (render as frost in our pipeline) — opaque Dark Bluish Gray.
- Turquoise or blue glass (stylisation; real glass is blue-grey).
- The second broadcast mast; the temporary green netting.

## Risks / open questions
1. **Height**: Scheme A tops at 96 plates (above the 60–80 brief) and needs an island ≥ 64 studs wide. Eric's call: A (proportion) vs B (78 plates, stubbier).
2. **Pod upper drum** is Ø 4 against a real 0.8 × Ø 6 = 4.9: the set-back will read deeper than real. Fallback: Ø 6 drum with the top two rows in a lighter colour.
3. **Pod bowl mount**: an upside-down 6×6 dish under a Ø 6 plate stack around a Ø 2 shaft — check the stud/anti-stud connection in the mould (`check-studs`) before building; `4285b` upside down is proven, `44375b` is not baked yet.
4. **Lock clip fit**: whether 1×1 clip tiles grip the spindles of `30055` must be checked in the mould (bar Ø); fallback: 1×1 round plates on the fence's top studs.
5. **Wall at minifig scale**: the real wall is 5–7 m (≈ 15–20 plates); compressed to ≈ 8 plates it may read as a garden wall. Check the parapet rhythm in a close-up.
6. **Season** (Eric's call): spring (azalea pink + forsythia yellow — but Kyoto is already the pink set) vs **autumn** (Namsan's maples; the plaza tree in `real-base-plaza.jpg`) vs summer green. Recommend autumn, colours kept below Kyoto's.
