### Assemblies (authoring scripts in git; `.ldr` files in `vendor/ldraw/asm/` are regenerated by them)
- `la_park` (`tools/ldraw/asm-la-park.mjs`, imports `asm-la-wheel.mjs`): the park section, 52 × 32. It holds the deck plates and planks, the wheel, the coaster oval and the railing. **Why one assembly:** the wheel stands inside the oval, and both stand on deck studs. As separate proxies their boxes overlap, and a proxy box cannot have plank tiles under it. The scene validator sees one box resting on the pile caps; `check-assemblies` and `check-asm-studs` see every brick. The scene retags the members by part id so `?only=wheel | coaster | pier` still isolates each element.
- `la_arch` and `la_sign66` (`tools/ldraw/asm-la-arch.mjs`). The arch's proxy uses a `mask`: only the two pylons occupy cells, so the planks run under the opening.

## 2. Technique per signature detail (v1–v3; round 4 changes in §6)
- **Wheel geometry** (SCHEME §1.3, built exactly):
  - The rim is a 20-gon of thin 5L liftarms in two alternating layers per rim, rims at |z| 1.5–2.5.
  - The drum is a 10-gon of 3L beams.
  - Every vertex carries a 7L axle. Spacer bushes and a Flat Silver cap bush sit on the side without a spoke (the 31389 joint dots).
  - Legs are 15L beams at 60° from the two lowest drum vertices to a Technic brick on a 1×2 brick. A 13L crossbar sits outside them at hole 10.
  - Hub 42.8 plates above the foot plates (scheme 42.7). Measured gaps: gondola bottom to the hanger below ≥ 0.10 stud; spoke axles to any foreign vertex axle ≥ 2.7.
- **Spokes (v2): thin Light Bluish Gray 10L axles, not 11L beams.** The 11L beams read as a heavy toy wheel (v1, `w1-front`). Each spoke runs through two axle/pin cross blocks whose pin holes turn on the vertex axles. The axle hole sits 1 stud off the vertex, so the spoke is the vertex-to-vertex line shifted 1 stud toward the hub. That keeps the rim-end blocks inside the rim and makes the lines near-radial, like the photo. Front spokes lean +27° and back spokes −27°.
- **Gondolas**, 20, Red / Yellow alternating, each hung level between the rims:
  - Hanger: an axle connector with its cross hole on the vertex axle and its socket pointing down. The vertex axle turns in the rim's round holes.
  - Below it: a 3L axle pole, a Light Bluish Gray 2×2 round-plate canopy turned 45° right under the hanger, 0.5 stud of air, and a tub: a 2×2 round plate under a 2×2 round tile (v3, no studs on top). The axle stub under the tub is the real boss.
- **Hub disc (v2 → v3).** A white 4×4 dish, dome out, front and back (v2 used a 6×6 round plate: 0.23 D and all studs; the judge asked for smaller and smooth). It sits on a bracket hung from the two lowest drum axles (10L): a 3L beam, a friction pin, then a 5L beam whose 4th hole sits 0.08 stud below the hub centre. A 2L axle runs through the dish's axle hole. Judge round 1 called the open drum "a hole".
- **West Coaster** (decision A):
  - Pieces: 4 × curve 90° R12, 2 × S-ramp (9 plates over 16 studs), 2 × straight 4. Bright Light Orange.
  - Height: the west (sea) turn is high and the east turn low.
  - Joints: 8 joints on grid lines. Each post is single-colour 2×2 round bricks (Red, Magenta, Bright Green, Blue repeating) with a matching 2×4 plate under both track ends. The track's tubes straddle its studs. A 2×2 tile in track colour sits across the end studs on top (v3: a plate there left studs on the running surface).
- **Deck:**
  - Reddish Brown 2-wide plates along the pier, staggered, spanning the bents.
  - Dark Tan planks across the pier, left out wherever something stands.
  - Dark Brown pile bents every 4 studs, 6 piles per bent on the park section (round bricks on the sea-bed plate through gaps in the water tiles, round brick + round plate on the sand), with a 1×8 / 1×6 cap.
  - Dark Blue railing: posts of two 1×1 round plates every 3 studs, tile handrail, 12-wide openings at the tail and the approach.
- **Arch:**
  - Dark Blue 1×2-brick pylons with a lower stripe group of five 1×2 plates (Bright Light Orange / Dark Blue).
  - The raised arch 1×12×3 stands on the inner studs, and the outer columns rise beside it with the upper stripes and a tile cap.
  - Cheese slopes on 1×1 plates smooth the mould's stepped shoulders (v2).
  - A white raised arch 1×6 is the crest panel. Tiles cover its top, the band's two free top studs and the four studs under the crest's arch (v3).
- **66 sign:** a Dark Bluish Gray post of 1×1 bricks and a 1×1 brick with a side stud carrying a white 2×2 round tile printed "66".
  - Print `route66`, design no. 12 in usa.md §1.12.4.
  - Lettering: highway-sign bold sans, condensed by scaling, black ring, black on white. Copy this choice into usa.md's lettering record.

## 3. Tried and rejected
- **11L thick beams as spokes** (v1, the scheme's first pick): the hole pattern and 0.9-stud width made a toy wheel. Replaced by axles (the scheme's own fallback, and how 31389 does it).
- **Spokes shifted outward** (v2a): the cross blocks poked out of the rim and the lines formed a star polygon. Shifted toward the hub instead.
- **Wheel and coaster as separate assemblies:** their proxy boxes overlap, and planks cannot sit under a proxy. Merged into `la_park`.
- **Turn centres at ±8 from the wheel** (the scheme): the high turn's bounding box ran through the lower rim and gondolas at 225°. Added a 4-stud straight at the high (west) end, so the oval is 48 long and off the wheel's centre by 2. The park deck grew from 48 to 52.
- **Medium Nougat on every 7th plank row** (the scheme's swatch test): read as orange stripes. Now one colour.
- **Trans-Clear water:** the renderer draws it as frost. Now Trans-Light-Blue over Sand Blue / Dark Blue, with a few trans plates as ripples and a white surf line.
- **A coaster car on the high 4-stud straight** (v2): its keel met the joint tile there. In round 4 two cars ride on the low east turn instead, clear of every joint (§6).
- **66 sign with a projected rectangle as its box:** the box crossed the planks, which sit 8 plates below the disc. The proxy is now the 1×1 post; the disc overhangs above everything near it.

## 4. Deviations from the SCHEME
- Spokes are axles in cross blocks, not 11L beams (fallback route). The lowest drum axles are 10L (legs + hub bracket).
- Hub disc added. The scheme listed it as unsolved; it is now solved with a bracket.
- Oval is 48 × 28 with a 4-straight at the west end, and the park deck is 52 × 32. The pier comes out 68 × 32 including tail, approach and landing.
- Water colour: Trans-Light-Blue, not Trans-Clear (see §3).
- No mid-ramp posts: the ramp has tubes only at its ends, so a post under the middle would clutch nothing.
- No lamps yet. The 66 sign is the disc only (the "SANTA MONICA" and "End of the Trail" bars cannot be read at this size).

## 5. Open issues
- **Judge v3 = 7.6 (gate 8.0).** Its top three: print the crest; put a car on the track and cover the post caps' exposed studs with round tiles; taper the gondola tub (2×2 round brick over an upside-down 2×2 dish) under a white dish canopy.
- **Crest panel unprinted.** "SANTA MONICA" would be print no. 13; usa.md §1.12.4 has 12. Eric decides: drop a print, or keep the crest plain.
- **Spoke density.** 20 lines against the real ≈ 60. More need more drum vertices, and a 20-gon drum would be 12.8 studs across.
- **Gondolas** (v4: tapered cone tubs) are still bounded by the 4-stud vertex pitch: about 3.3 studs below the pivot.
- **The coaster is single-level.** The real coaster crosses itself; a second level needs a longer deck.
- **Proxy file size:** solved in round 4 (`--hull`, 16.4 MB → 0.9 KB).
- **Shared scripts touched**, all append-style (check with the lead):
  - `check-asm-studs.ts`: an axle/pin-through-hole rule and BY_DESIGN additions.
  - `check-assemblies.ts`: BARS additions, plus INTERLOCK pairs for track × joint plates and raised arch × shoulder slopes.
  - `bake.mjs` LDCOLOR: 1 Blue, 272 Dark Blue, 84 Medium Nougat.
- **The test-scene port was not 8131.** Another worktree's server (lego-paris) held 8131, so renders were served from **8141**.
