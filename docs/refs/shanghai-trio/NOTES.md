# Lujiazui trio (三件套): Shanghai Tower · SWFC · Jin Mao — LEGO references

Collected 2026-10-02. Part names are types, not numbers, unless the source gave one (numbers checked against the vendored LDraw library where noted).
Target in our set: Shanghai Tower ≈ 84 plates, SWFC ≈ 66, Jin Mao ≈ 56, backdrop behind the Oriental Pearl.

Older refs used here (not copied): `../shanghai-21039/tbb-16-*`, `tbb-17-*`, `tbb-23-*` (21039, The Brothers Brick); `../shanghai-moc/nexogon-*.jpg`; `../shanghai-real/Jin_Mao_Tower.jpg`, `Shanghai_World_Financial_Center.jpg`.

## 0. Real towers — proportions to hit

**How I measured.** Pixel readings on the Commons photos below, on a 50 px grid overlay (scratch only), using the far, telephoto shots where possible (`real-group-bund-1.jpg` at 3840 px, `real-shtower-2.jpg`, `real-swfc-1.jpg`, `../shanghai-real/Jin_Mao_Tower.jpg`). Street-level shots shrink the tops (perspective), so I don't use their H:W ratios. Heights come from Wikipedia infoboxes. I couldn't find a published base width for Shanghai Tower or Jin Mao, so those ratios come from the photos only and could be off by ±10 %.

Files (Wikimedia Commons, downscaled with `sips -Z 1400`):
`real-shtower-1/2/3.jpg`, `real-swfc-1.jpg` (aerial, wide face), `real-swfc-2.jpg` (oblique, curved face), `real-swfc-top.jpg` (aperture close-up), `real-jinmao-1/2.jpg`, `real-jinmao-closeup.jpg` (tier joints), `real-group-bund-1.jpg` and `real-group-bund-2.jpg` (from the Bund), `real-group-trio.jpg` and `real-group-trio-2.jpg` (from the street in Pudong).

- **Shanghai Tower (上海中心)**: 632 m architectural, 587.4 m roof, 128 floors.
  - Plan: a **rounded triangle** (outer skin) around circular floor plates. One apex has a **V-notch** that runs the full height. Nine stacked zones of 12–15 floors each, split by double-height refuge/belt-truss floors. Those floors show as **thin light horizontal bands**: 8 bands, so 9 zones. The bands get closer together towards the top because the zones shrink with the taper.
  - Twist: **120° over the height** (Gensler/Wikipedia), so about 13° per zone. Seen from one side, the notch makes a long shallow S-curve that crosses about a third of the face (`real-group-trio.jpg`, `real-shtower-3.jpg`).
  - Taper: sources say "scaling ~55 %". Measured top-drum width / near-base width = 0.63 (`real-shtower-2`) and 0.57 (Bund view). The silhouette is **convex** (entasis): it bulges a little at mid-height, then narrows into the crown.
  - H : W(base) ≈ **6.5–7 : 1**. The visible part alone is ≥ 6.1 : 1 with the bottom hidden. **At 84 plates (33.6 studs) the true ratio gives a base of ~5 studs and a top of ~3. The planned 7 studs is ~1.4× too wide**, about 4.8 : 1, which is the same squat ratio 21039 uses (~5.5 : 1 measured on `tbb-17`). Decide this before building.
  - Crown: the top ~7 % is an open-frame drum, with the rim cut on a slant that rises towards the notch side. No spire.
  - Colour: pale silver-blue glass that reflects the sky, with whitish bands. LEGO candidates: **Light Bluish Gray / Sand Blue body, White or Light Bluish Gray bands, Trans-Clear accents**. Not Medium Blue.
- **Shanghai World Financial Center (环球金融中心, "bottle opener")**: 492 m architectural, 487.4 m roof, 101 floors.
  - Geometry: a **58 m square prism cut by two sweeping arcs**. The plan is a square at the base and shrinks to a **single line along the diagonal** at the top. So from the wide side (the side with the hole, seen from the Bund) the silhouette is an **almost straight-sided slab** about one diagonal (~82 m) wide. From 90° away it **tapers to a blade**. The two curved faces cross the wide face as a long **diagonal crease**: from one bottom corner to the opposite top corner, curving (`real-swfc-2.jpg`, `real-swfc-1.jpg`).
  - H : W(wide face) ≈ **6.0 : 1** (geometry 492/82; photo 6.3). At 66 plates (26.4 studs) that is ~4.4 studs wide.
  - Aperture: a trapezoid, **wider at the top**. Averaged over 3 photos: top width ≈ **0.70 W**, bottom width ≈ **0.55 W**, height ≈ **0.42 W**, solid beam above ≈ **0.18–0.2 W**. The opening fills the top ~3–10 % of the height. The observation bridge spans the bottom of the hole (100F, 474 m). On a 4–5-stud face: about 3 studs at the top, 2–3 at the bottom, ~6 plates tall, with a 3-plate beam above.
  - Colour: steel blue-grey glass with fine horizontal mullions; the aperture rim reads silver. LEGO candidates: **Sand Blue or Trans-Light Blue over Dark Bluish Gray; Light Bluish Gray / Flat Silver rim**. 21039's Medium Blue was called "not especially accurate" (BrickArchitect).
- **Jin Mao Tower (金茂大厦)**: 420.5 m to the tip, 385.5 m roof, 35 m spire, 88 floors.
  - Plan: a square with **stepped corners**, which reads as near-octagonal. The faces carry **vertical fins / mullions in silver aluminium** over dark glass, plus a strong central rib on each face (`real-jinmao-closeup.jpg`).
  - Setbacks: "16 segments, each 1/8 shorter than the 16-storey base"; the first 4 segments hold floors 1–50. Measured tier heights from `Jin_Mao_Tower.jpg` (px, base hidden): 203 → 175 → 125 → 105 → 85 → 60 → 50 → 30 → 30. That is roughly ×0.85 per tier, so **about 10 readable tiers**, then the crown. At each joint the tier's top **flares out slightly** (a pagoda eave) and the next tier starts a little narrower at the corners. The overall silhouette is **nearly parallel**: top shoulders ≈ 0.88 × base width. The "pagoda" comes from the **rhythm of the joints**, not from a big taper.
  - Crown and spire: a steep stepped pyramid ≈ 0.45 W tall, then a fin cluster and needle spire ≈ 0.4 W. Together they are ~14 % of the height to the tip.
  - H : W ≈ **6 : 1 to the tip, 5.2 : 1 to the shoulders**. At 56 plates (22.4 studs) that is ~4 studs wide: about 48 plates of tiers, ~4 plates of crown, ~4 plates of spire.
  - Colour: silver-grey lattice over dark blue-grey glass, warm at sunset. LEGO candidates: **Light Bluish Gray fins/ribs, Dark Bluish Gray glass, Flat Silver / Pearl Gray spire**.
- **Group view from Puxi** (`real-group-bund-1.jpg`, `real-group-bund-2.jpg`):
  - Left to right as seen from the Bund: **Jin Mao stands directly in front of SWFC**. Its spire tip lands **inside SWFC's aperture**, and its body hides SWFC's left half. **Shanghai Tower stands clear to the right**, about one SWFC-width of sky away, with no overlap.
  - Oriental Pearl is far left and in front. Because it is closer to the camera, its upper ball sits at ~0.85 of ST height in the picture and its antenna rises above ST's top.
  - Height steps in the picture: Jin Mao tip ≈ 0.70, SWFC roof ≈ 0.78, ST 1.0 (true ratio 0.665 : 0.78 : 1). Planned 56 / 66 / 84 plates = 0.67 : 0.79 : 1 ✓.
  - The aperture faces roughly east–west, so from the Bund it **does read as a hole with sky behind it**. Orient SWFC's wide face to the camera.

## 1. LEGO Architecture 21039 Shanghai (official, 2018, 597 pcs) — ST + SWFC, no Jin Mao

URLs: https://brickarchitect.com/2018/review-21039-shanghai-skyline/ · https://brickset.com/article/39548/review-21039-shanghai · https://www.brothers-brick.com/2017/12/31/lego-architecture-21309-shanghai-skyline-review/ · inventory https://brickset.com/inventories/21039-1
Files: `lego21039-shtower-brickset.jpg`, `lego21039-shtower-liftarm-stack-brickset.jpg`, `lego21039-swfc-brickset.jpg` (+ `../shanghai-21039/tbb-16`, `tbb-17`, `tbb-23`)

- **ST twist:** a stack of **57 thin 3×3 quarter-circle liftarms** (Brickset inventory 65125 "halfbeam curve 3×3" = LDraw **32249**, Technic Beam 3×3×0.5 Liftarm Bent 90 Quarter Circle). They come in **Dark Bluish Gray**, with **Light Bluish Gray** ones as the zone bands (8 bands). The stack is threaded on a **central Technic axle** and **two flexible axles** (32199 Flex 11L, 32202 Flex 16L, black) through the end holes. The flex axles set the gradual rotation; the whole stack is twisted by hand, then locked. The D-shaped liftarm reads as a rounded triangle with the notch side flat.
- ST taper: none worth mentioning. The width is constant; the twist alone carries the shape. Base: a light-grey podium of curved slopes.
- **SWFC:** Medium Blue **1×3 two-stud jumper plates** (34103, new in this set, 32 pcs) and 1×2 jumpers, faced with **2×3 / 2×4 tiles**. The curved faces become **stepped tile panels**: each tier is half a stud narrower than the one below, which makes a chevron of steps. The **aperture** is a gap between two columns under a full-width beam. **Left/right 2×4 wedge plates** (41769/41770, Medium Blue, in the inventory) are the likely source of the slanted trapezoid sides (`tbb-23` top view). That attribution is mine, not the reviewers'.
- Reads well: the ST twist is "a really clever solution" (BrickArchitect). The bands read clearly, and the dark grey helps it stand apart from SWFC.
- Fails: the ST is too stubby (~5.5 : 1). The SWFC's stair-steps "don't capture the shape" (Brickset); BrickArchitect called it his "least favorite building", and the colour is too saturated.
- **Lesson:** a thin rounded-triangle profile stacked on an axle, with each layer turned a few degrees, is the official answer for the twist. The bands are the same profile in a lighter colour. SWFC needs something better than stepped tiles for the curved faces.

## 2. LEGOLAND Discovery Centre Shanghai — Miniland Lujiazui (2016, 10 master builders, 8,531 h, 3.9 M bricks for the whole Miniland)

URLs: https://www.whb.cn/zhuzhan/kandian/20160321/52537.html (facts) · photos via https://post.smzdm.com/p/axdpewld/ (sourced from Xiaohongshu)
Files: `ldc-shanghai-miniland-lujiazui-1.jpg`, `ldc-shanghai-miniland-lujiazui-2.jpg`

- ST: a smooth **stacked cylinder-like shaft** with a fine studs-out / tile texture. The dark **zone bands** are recessed rings: 8 of them, evenly spaced, clearly the signature at a distance. The twist shows only as a slight S in the silhouette, and the notch is almost absent. The convex taper is good.
- SWFC: **trans-light-blue / trans-clear** (lit from inside) with the trapezoid hole near the top and a bright rim. The blade taper is visible from the oblique view (`-2`).
- Jin Mao: tan/silver with a stepped crown and spire. The tiers read as fine horizontal ribs; the joints are weakly expressed.
- **Lesson:** at backdrop distance, **bands + taper** carry the Shanghai Tower and the **hole + slab** carry SWFC. A twist that only shows as a slight S is acceptable. Lighting is theirs, not ours: our trans colours must work unlit.

## 3. LEGOLAND Shanghai Resort — Miniland (2025)

URLs: https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30985314 · https://www.jiemian.com/article/13006691.html · Xinhua gallery https://www.news.cn/photo/20250705/07b86eb39e5447a990f9db1fc45b59fc/c.html

- Visible in the photos: ST in white/trans-clear with **pink/lilac lit zone bands** and a clearly **twisted, tapering silhouette** with the notch line. SWFC in trans-green/teal light with the aperture. I can't pick out Jin Mao in either shot; it may be hidden behind the SWFC/foreground towers.
- **Not verified:** no source describes how the 10 m twist is built, and I found no daytime close-up.
- **Lesson:** even LEGO's own masters keep the trio as **glass bodies + strong bands**. Treat this as a proportion and silhouette reference only.

## 4. Nexogon Shanghai Tower — Li Li (MOC Recipes), New Elementary 2017

URL: https://www.newelementary.com/2017/05/nexogon-shanghai-tower.html
Files: `nexogon-twist-base-view.jpg` (+ `../shanghai-moc/nexogon-shanghai-tower.jpg`, `nexogon-section.jpg`)

- Core: the hexagonal **"Nexogon" plate** (27255 = LDraw "Plate 6×6 Hexagonal with Pin Hole and Handles") makes a triangle with a concentric centre. **Two nexogons stacked, one pin hole up and one down**, join the sections through the centre hole, so each section can **rotate freely on the central pin**.
- Twist: **15 triangular sections × 8° = ~120°**, matching the real tower. Each section is **4 studs tall** because the centre Technic hole has an odd height. Also mentioned: the Technic rotor with 3 blades (32125), the holder ring with 3 bars (20612), bars and clips.
- Facade: rounded-triangle walls of **curved bricks / curved slopes laid around the triangle**, light grey with **dark grey stripes** as the bands. Sections step back slightly for the taper (`nexogon-shanghai-tower.jpg`). The facade method is my reading of the photos; the article doesn't spell it out.
- Reads well: the real 120° twist, the triangular plan and the stepped taper all read at once.
- Fails: each 4-stud section shows as a **visible drum with a ledge**, so it looks segmented, not smooth. "Difficult to transport, as everything rotates" (the article). It is also far bigger than our ~7 studs.
- **Lesson:** the rotate-on-a-pin-per-section idea transfers. **Our version: ~9 sections (one per real zone) × ~13°, each section a rounded-triangle plate stack on a centre axle/pin, each a few % smaller, a light band plate between sections.** This needs real-geometry checks because the corners overhang the section below.

## 5. Spencer R (Spencer Rezkalla) — microscale SWFC + Jin Mao, 2013 (~1 : 650)

URLs: https://www.brothers-brick.com/2013/04/02/reaching-for-the-sky-in-chin · https://www.eurobricks.com/forum/forums/topic/81047-moc-shanghai-world-financial-center · Flickr https://www.flickr.com/photos/51130204@N04/8607145488/
Files: `spencerR-swfc-jinmao-micro-1.jpg` (both towers), `spencerR-swfc-micro-2.jpg` (from above), `spencerR-swfc-aperture-closeup.jpg`, `spencerR-swfc-side-blade.jpg`

- SWFC: **11 × 11-stud footprint, ~76 cm tall**. Almost all **trans-clear plates** with a **blue inner core** showing through (one plate = one floor). The **diagonal crease is made by stepping the plate outline one stud per few floors**, so the curved faces emerge as a fine staircase seen through the glass. The aperture's sloped sides are **trans-clear slopes**. The beam above is a row of trans-clear **round-hole / tile plates**, with a white observation bridge across the hole (close-up). From the side the slab narrows to a true **blade** (`side-blade`).
- Jin Mao: light grey. The **corner piers step inward at every tier**, with a dark recess at each joint. The **vertical ribs** come from grille tiles / 1×1 columns, and the corner fins flare at the joints. The crown is a stepped pyramid with a slim antenna spire. **The best Jin Mao setbacks found.**
- Fails: SWFC is a bit too milky (all trans-clear) and its trapezoid is shallow. The whole build is huge for us.
- **Lesson:** SWFC's curves are just **an outline that changes a little every few plates**. In plates that is a stepped diagonal, which reads as a curve from a distance. Jin Mao = **corner piers that step in at each tier + a flared joint band**.

## 6. Mengfan LEGO MOC — the whole trio + podiums (digital renders, "80,000+ pieces")

URL: https://post.smzdm.com/p/axdpewld/ (reposted from Xiaohongshu; piece count is that post's claim, not verified; the renders say "Copyright © Mengfan LEGO MOC")
Files: `moc80k-trio-render-front.jpg`, `moc80k-trio-render-aerial.jpg`, `moc80k-trio-render-low.jpg`

- **Proportions are faithful.** Height ratios measured on the front render: ST/SWFC = 1.28 (real 1.28), Jin Mao/SWFC = 0.85 (real 0.85). Shows the real site plan with the Super Brand / podium buildings.
- ST: a **trans-clear facade over a grey frame**, one ring per floor. The **twist is built by offsetting each ring a little**, so the **notch line spirals clearly** (`aerial`, `low`). There are **dark bands at the zone floors** and a convex taper, and the crown is an open frame with a slanted rim. The best ST silhouette in this collection.
- SWFC: **trans-light-blue / Sand-Blue-looking glass**, the trapezoid aperture with a white bridge, and the **diagonal crease made as a stepped seam** down the face.
- Jin Mao: grey. **About 9 tiers with dark joint bands**, the upper tiers pulled in to a **pagoda-like convex crown**, and a fin-and-spire top. It is chunky: about 5 : 1, wider than real.
- **Lesson:** the target look for all three. **ST: glass rings + dark zone bands + a visible spiral notch. SWFC: blue glass slab + stepped crease + clear hole. Jin Mao: tiers with dark joints.** Its piece count is ~40× our budget, so we borrow the reading, not the method.

## 7. SPBrix (LesDiy) Jin Mao Tower — MOC-148580, 1 : 800, 1,611 pcs, 12.5 × 54 × 12.5 cm

URL: https://www.merlinsbricks.com/sets/lesdiy-3573871/
File: `spbrix-jinmao-moc148580.jpg` (render)

- Square shaft in Light Bluish Gray with **Dark Bluish Gray bands at each tier joint**: 6 bands on the lower shaft, closer together higher up. The faces are **SNOT tile / grille panels** with a centre rib.
- Corner **"wing" fins** (angled plates / wedge pieces) flare at the upper joints and at the crown shoulder. The crown is a stepped dark-grey pyramid with fin clusters and a needle spire.
- Fails: the shaft is **straight and too uniform**. The corners don't step in tier by tier, so it reads as a banded box. The setbacks only appear in the top third.
- **Lesson:** flared corner fins at the joints are a cheap, readable "pagoda eave". The corner set-ins must still be built on every tier, or the pagoda is lost.

## Borrow / avoid

**Shanghai Tower**
- Borrow: a **rounded-triangle profile stacked on a central axle, each layer turned a few degrees**. Use 21039's thin quarter-circle liftarm stack (32249) for a small build, or Nexogon-style rotating sections, one per zone (~9 × 13° = 120°). Add **8 light (or dark) band layers** at the zone breaks (21039, LDC, Mengfan). A **gentle convex taper** to ~0.6× at the top. An **open crown ring with a slanted rim**. Pale silver/sand-blue/clear, not mid blue.
- Avoid: constant width (21039). Visible 4-stud drums with ledges (Nexogon). A 7-stud base at 84 plates if we want the real ~6.5 : 1. **Decide: true slender ≈ 5 studs, or 21039-squat 7 studs.**

**SWFC**
- Borrow: a **wide face to the camera, a narrow blade to the side**. The curved faces as an **outline that steps one plate-edge at a time** (Spencer R, Mengfan). A **trapezoid hole ~0.7 W at the top / 0.55 W at the bottom / 0.42 W tall, under a ~3-plate beam**, with **wedge plates or slopes for the slanted sides** (21039, Spencer R). A thin bridge across the bottom of the hole. Sand Blue / trans-light-blue glass, silver rim.
- Avoid: broad stair-stepped tile chevrons (21039: "least favorite"). All-trans-clear milkiness. Saturated Medium Blue.

**Jin Mao**
- Borrow: **~9–10 tiers, each ~0.85× the height of the one below**. **Corner piers that step in at each tier**, with a **flared joint band / corner fins** as the pagoda eave (Spencer R, SPBrix). A centre rib on each face from vertical grille / 1×1 columns. A **stepped-pyramid crown + fin cluster + needle spire ≈ 14 % of the height**. Silver-grey ribs on dark glass.
- Avoid: a straight banded box (SPBrix lower shaft). Squat proportions (Mengfan ~5 : 1; real ~6 : 1 to the tip). **Placement: Jin Mao in front of SWFC as seen from the Bund, its spire in SWFC's hole.** Keep that overlap.
